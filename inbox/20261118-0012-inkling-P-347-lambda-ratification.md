**Is the dial as designed ratifiable as-is?** Yes — with changes that are all documentation/spec, not measurement:
1. **Ship an in-band value as convention, and keep 0.40.** It sits in the band ([0.3902, 0.4032) ∋ 0.40); it is the shipped default; no available estimator can distinguish any in-band pair, so changing to the midpoint 0.3967 buys literally nothing measurable and costs every receipt, test and doc citation. 2. **Make "maximum flexibility for the dial" concrete, or it is a slogan:** the flexibility lives in the **dynamics-registry pattern**, not in the constant — every context override (personnel, rivalry, playoffs, opponent structure, key players, aggressiveness, dominant groups) is a registry entry that (a) names its context variables, (b) carries its own held-out-season receipt beating fixed-λ₀ (R-6's own acceptance bar), (c) rides pooled-REG λ₀ until its cell resolves (~541 games; POST never does — POST rides λ₀ by construction). An override that cannot name its held-out receipt does not enter the product. That is what fluctuating-by-context means operationally: each fluctuation is itself measured, not declared.
3. **λ₀ stays one named constant in one place** (engine constants, with the band and R-6 beside it), and the product never prints λ to the user — the flexibility is a build freedom, not a user-facing dial; no user decision plumbs it (consistent with "maximum flexibility" being the architect's constraint, not the user's control).

**If the estimator cannot measure λ in-band, what estimator can — and is building one worth a round?**
- The window math says the current estimator is not the only possible one. A **pooled partial-pooling (empirical-Bayes) estimator** — λ_c = λ₀ + δ_c, δ_c shrunk by cell n toward 0, POST cells never pool with REG, margin cells flagged MARGINAL — is the right shape and is the open work R-6 names ("the open work is the estimator"; the direction-innovation prompt asks exactly this: "Is the pooled / partially-pooled estimator (λ_c = λ₀ + δ_c, empirical-Bayes shrinkage, POST never pools with REG) the right estimator?"). Per-cell SE ≤ 0.0046 is the resolution bar; pooled REG clears it ~4.9×; POST never does and must not pretend to.
- **Is building it worth a round? Recommend: yes, but scoped — and honest about what it buys.** It is a Lab job (the Lab already runs policy→outcome calibration), not an Architect engine build; the engine ships λ₀ until a context entry beats fixed-λ on held-out seasons. Acceptance: the pooled estimator recovers λ₀ within the window on REG holdout (bias and SE reported), a context cell enters the registry only with its held-out win, POST rides REG λ₀ always. **Not worth it:** chasing per-margin-cell λ (the resolution is MARGINAL and the value of the insight is below the noise); any work that treats POST as resolvable (it is not). **And it is not worth a round for v1's shipped value** — v1 ships the convention (0.40) regardless; the estimator round buys the *flexibility* half of the ruling. If the Owner wants the dial to actually flex in v1.1, the estimator is the gate; if he is content with a fixed in-band λ₀ for v1, defer it to the dynamics-registry round.

If the Owner wants the number right, MUSE should publish the derivation; do not ratify any constant derived from a figure nobody can reproduce.

**What does "maximum flexibility for the dial" mean concretely for the value the product ships?** One in-band convention (0.40), the band recorded beside it, and zero user-facing surface; flexibility arrives only as measured, held-out-season-beating context overrides in the dynamics registry, pooled-REG-backed, POST on λ₀; every override names its receipt or it does not ship.
