## Part B — Structural side (real-roster bias)

From slots.ts: in 3-4, DT2 (weight 300) is re-rated as ILB (dest ILB, label ILB2). So a 4-3 roster's DT2 becomes a 3-4 ILB2, and the flex 4-3→3-4 routes through DT2-from-OLB at 6. The bias (28-3 split, +1144 mean) is driven by real rosters being 4-3-shaped: more DT/DE bodies than OLB bodies, so 3-4's OLB1/WILL and OLB2/SAM and ILB2/DT2 slots are manned by flexed players.

Proposal B1 — Rate ILB2 off WILL (weight 150) instead of DT2 (weight 300). Rationale: ILB2 in a 3-4 is a lighter, more mobile player than DT2; rating it off DT2 inflates its value and makes the flex cost 12×300=3600 for DL5-from-ILB. Rating off WILL (150) halves the stand-in cost. Effect on real bias: INFERRED lower (fewer high-cost stand-ins), but changes a slot-table fact — Owner/Architect decision, not mine. Effect on synthetic gap: DL5-from-ILB becomes 12×150=1800→900, 3-4 loss 1800→900, gap widens to 1600 (worse). So B1 alone worsens the synthetic gap even as it may reduce real bias. Trade-off: real bias vs synthetic gap.

Proposal B2 — Keep DT2 as ILB2 (current) but acknowledge the bias is structural: real rosters are 4-3-shaped, and no flex re-pricing closes a bias driven by body types. The "larger change" would be a roster-generation or slot-table change that gives 3-4 rosters more natural OLB bodies — that is a product-level decision (ratings generation, Owner's alone) or a slot-table change (Architect). I am not proposing either; flagging.
