## Findings

1. **Arithmetic inconsistency: WR blocking value.** The work claims "Elite WR vs Average WR: +0.05 to +0.10 EPA per run play" but the proposed constant `WR_BLOCK_VALUE_PER_OVR = 0.002` gives only +0.03 EPA per run play for a 15-OVR-point spread (15 × 0.002 = 0.03). The math doesn't support the claim. Also, `runGameEPA += (avgWRBlockGrade - 50) * 0.006` gives +0.15 EPA/run play for a 75-grade WR (25 × 0.006 = 0.15), which exceeds the claimed 0.05-0.10 range. **Two different formulas give two different magnitudes.** Recommend: pick one formula, verify the magnitude against the PFF "perfectly blocked" study (0.54 EPA swing per negative block), and document which is authoritative. The current inconsistency means the Architect can't tell which to use.

2. **QB rushing: minor inconsistency between two estimates.** The work estimates "0.5-1.0 EPA/season per OVR point" and "0.8 EPA per season per OVR point" (two slightly different numbers). And `QB_RUSH_VALUE_PER_OVR = 0.025` (per rush attempt) with 50 rushes/season and 20 OVR points above 70 gives 0.025 × 20 × 50 = 25 EPA/season (top of the 15-25 range). But "0.8 EPA per season per OVR point" gives 20 × 0.8 = 16 EPA/season. Both are in range but differ by ~50%. Recommend: document which is the primary estimate and which is the cross-check. Minor (both are in range).

3. **Evidence labels are too generous.** The work labels everything as "VERIFIED (PFF published grades/studies, ESPN QBR methodology, nflWAR arXiv paper, Pro Football Techs PFF data)". But the calibration (converting EPA to OVR points) is INFERRED/CALCULATED, not directly sourced. The sources provide EPA values; the OVR-point conversion is derived. Per §7, derived estimates must be labelled as "calculated" or "inferred", not "verified". Recommend: split the evidence into (a) sourced facts (VERIFIED: PFF grades, EPA/play values) and (b) derived estimates (CALCULATED: OVR-point conversion, proposed constants). The HOW TO CHECK already says "Architect calibrates drive model run game to match league averages (P-205)" — good, that acknowledges the need for validation.

4. **"QB rush attempt share of offensive EPA: ~10-15%" is an estimate, not a sourced figure.** The work derives this from nflWAR (QB = 67.5% of offensive EPA, rush subset). The "10-15%" is an estimate of the rush subset share. Not directly sourced. Should be labelled as INFERRED with the derivation shown. Flag.

5. **The proposed constants lack units and validation.** `QB_RUSH_VALUE_PER_OVR = 0.025` (EPA per rush attempt per OVR point) and `WR_BLOCK_VALUE_PER_OVR = 0.002` (EPA per run play per OVR point). These are proposed starting points. The work says "Architect calibrates drive model run game to match league averages (P-205)". Good — validation is planned. But the constants should be clearly marked as "proposed/estimate, pending calibration" so the Architect knows not to treat them as verified.

6. **No conflict with existing data.** The QB rushing and WR blocking values are new estimates for the drive model. They don't conflict with any existing protected constants (DEFAULT_WEIGHTS, FLEX, etc.) or existing data. They're additive. Good.

7. **SAFE ALONE: N/A** (data document, not product code). The Architect decides whether to adopt. The work is advisory evidence, not a code change.
