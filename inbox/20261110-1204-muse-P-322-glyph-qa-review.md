## Design assessment (INFERRED — the design is unmeasured, so this is structural review)

4. **The Vjob value layer is genuinely different — and untested.** Glyph derives job value from duty-load (`Vjob[j] = Σ w_d · NEED[j,d]`, plus a gravity term) instead of from slot weights (the guard) or engine-measured slot values. This is his answer to the "no Owner weights for other sports" tension, and it is structurally distinct from every other design in the field. But it is **speculative**: Vjob from duty-load may not reproduce the slot-value ladder. The risk case is C itself — C's job has SNP 10 (a maximum duty load), so C's Vjob could be high, which would make `Gv(C,OT)` small — the opposite of what the Owner's Q11 answer needs (C>OT = 12). Glyph says Gv(C,OT) "becomes the dominant term → a large penalty (≈ cap)" but that is INFERRED, not measured, and the duty-load logic points the other way for C (high SNP load → high Vjob → small guard). **Before this value layer can be adopted, it needs a measurement:** compute Vjob for all roles from a calibrated football pack and check whether the resulting guard reproduces C>OT = 12 and the rest of the value ladder. Until then it is an idea, not a verified layer.
5. **The CORE/prohibition mechanism is sound in structure.** Defining prohibition as "a CORE duty the source supplies at zero" (or R ≥ T_forbid) is the same mechanism Inkling uses (derived prohibitions, §2.6) and it satisfies Q15's "mostly forbidden + some formula-priced" without a hand-written list. Glyph's version adds the CORE set explicitly, which is cleaner than Inkling's NEED ≥ 9 threshold (a CORE set is authored per job; a threshold is a magic number). This is a genuine contribution — if the design is ever built, the CORE set is the better prohibition mechanism.
6. **Gravity as duties + value amplification is the most complete gravity treatment in the field.** Glyph puts GRV_O/GRV_D in the duty vocabulary (so "can you provide that gravity in this job?" prices inside A) AND in Vjob (so gravity spots are expensive to cheap-fill). Inkling has a hook (γ_g, default 0); VL places gravity on G; MUSE-D2 folds it into the guard's measured weights. Glyph's two-channel treatment is the most thorough. It is unmeasured (no gravity data exists yet), but the structure is right.
7. **The sport-pack architecture is the right shape for portability.** Roles, duty vocabulary, NEED/HAVE/CORE/Vjob per sport, one formula — this is the G-PORT framework Glyph proposed earlier, now aligned to the Q&A. It is the cleanest portability story in the field (no Owner weights, no per-sport formula changes). The open question is only whether the Vjob layer (point 4) actually works.

## What is good (no correction needed)

8. **Q&A mapping is complete and faithful.** Inputs (OVR + preferred position, no per-player stats, no intangibles), two ingredients, malleable combination, no named exceptions/floor/pairs, continuous raw inside + simple integers outside, dual free, positions-not-in-the-game priced from the job definition, DL5-as-blitzer from a NEED profile, scale deferred. Every hard answer is addressed.
9. **The self-consistency constraint (NEED[j_r] ⪯ HAVE[r] for a role's own job) is a clean replacement for pairs.** It states the invariant as a check, not a patch — this is the right way to handle the Q12 pair problem at the architecture level.
10. **The SS2 worked example is sensible.** SS → SS2 near-free, ILB/OLB moderate, FS low, CB moderate, OL/DL/QB forbidden or at-cap. The shape matches Inkling-Q1's and MUSE-D2's measured SS2 results (DBs in cheaply, big men at 12). INFERRED (not run), but consistent with the measured designs.

## Bottom line

G-QA-D1 is the most architecturally complete design in the field (sport packs, CORE/prohibition, two-channel gravity, self-consistency instead of pairs) — but it is anchored to the **retired REV 4 table** (107 rules, QB>FS at 10) instead of the live 99, it carries a REV 4 artifact (QB→FS) into its verification checklist, and its distinctive value layer (Vjob from duty-load) is **unmeasured and possibly backwards for C** (C's SNP 10 load could make C's Vjob high, shrinking the C>OT guard). Re-anchor to the live 99, remove the QB>FS reference, and measure Vjob against a calibrated football pack before adopting the value layer. The architecture is worth keeping; the evidence anchor and the value layer need work.
