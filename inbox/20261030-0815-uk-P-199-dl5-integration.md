## DL5 SPECIFICATION SUMMARY (VERIFIED from ENGINE-MATH-SPEC §12)

### 12.1 What It Is
- **30th starting spot** (after 29 spots in REV 3, before TE3/WR4 in REV 5)
- **DL5** = rotational fifth defensive lineman
- **Natural roles**: DT and EDGE (penalty 0)
- **Snap share**: ~33% of defensive snaps (measured: 1,632 team-games 2023-2025, nflverse; mean 32.8%, median 35%, middle half 22%-44%)
- **Source**: `results/dl5-snap-measurement-2026-10-27.txt`, script `dl5-snaps.py`
- **Caveat**: 3-4 edge rushers labeled LB not counted

### 12.2 Flex Rules (Derived from Owner's Guide, not invented)

| EDGE | 0 | Natural |

| ILB (MIKE) | -4 | Guide allows MIKE→DT |

**Implementation**: `flexdl5.mjs` re-derives from `flex.json` — cannot drift.

### 12.3 Engine Integration (Exact Literals)
**File**: `results/engine-patch-2026-10-27-dl5.js.txt`

**Mechanics**:
- No new matchup code
- DL5 on field replaces **one of DE1, DE2, DT1, DT2** equally likely (25% each, INFERRED)
- Averaged over all snaps (33% share):
  - Each of 4 starters' weights × (1 - 0.33/4) = × 0.9175
  - DL5 weight = 0.33 × mean(DE1, DE2, DT1, DT2)
  - Total weight unchanged
- Applied to: `passRush`, `frontRun`, `frontBase`, `frontNick`
- `STATIC_SLOTS` (starter-only layer) **unchanged on purpose**
- Same pattern as DIME (fixed share, not a package)
