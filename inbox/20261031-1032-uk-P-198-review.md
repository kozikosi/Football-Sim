## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The document is a **strong, honest, and well-structured proposal** that correctly identifies the tension between "one HTML file" (AGENTS.md §3) and "interview-grade project." The staged path (0→7) is logical, the senior engineer's questions are realistic, and the learning path is actionable. 

**Key improvements needed:**
1. **Stage 0 is not actually "done"** — Owner approvals for ENGINE-MATH-SPEC are pending (P-004, P-007, flex REV 4)
2. **Stage 1 (RNG) is mis-scoped** — it says "inside V23" but V23 is Colors V1 which is already building on a separate branch
3. **Missing: Data migration strategy** — 1,200-player dataset → new Madden ratings (Owner 28 Oct)
4. **Missing: Performance budgets** — 7.8ms/game is measured but no SLO/SLA defined
5. **Missing: Accessibility compliance** — Owner mandates `prefers-reduced-motion`, WCAG contrast
6. **Stage 4 framework decision** — SvelteKit vs Next.js needs concrete criteria, not just "lean"
7. **Stage 7 "second sport"** — GPT's multisport branch should be reviewed NOW, not at Stage 7

### 6. STAGE 4 FRAMEWORK DECISION — NEEDS CRITERIA, NOT JUST "LEAN" (Line 30)

**Current:** "My lean: SvelteKit. Decide at Stage 4, not now."

**Evidence (REPORTED/INFERRED):**
- Next.js on Cloudflare: "OpenNext adapter on Workers rather than Pages, limits: Node middleware unsupported, 3 MiB compressed Worker size on free plan"
- SvelteKit: "smaller and quicker to learn and pairs cleanly with Cloudflare"

**Missing:** Decision matrix with weighted criteria:
| Criterion | Weight | Next.js | SvelteKit | Astro | Vanilla+Vite |
|-----------|--------|---------|-----------|-------|--------------|
| Cloudflare Workers support | High | Partial (OpenNext) | Native | Native | Native |
| Bundle size (engine + UI) | High | ~200KB+ | ~50KB | ~30KB | ~10KB |
| Learning curve | Medium | Medium | Low | Low | N/A |
| Hiring signal | High | High | Medium | Low | Low |
| SEO/SSR need | Low | Yes | Yes | Yes | No |
| TypeScript integration | High | Excellent | Excellent | Good | Good |

**Recommendation:** Define criteria now, decide at Stage 4 with evidence.
