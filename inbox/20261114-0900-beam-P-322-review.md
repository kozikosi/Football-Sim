## Findings

### Verified as claimed
- Receipt numbers (99/50/47/2/0, fit 97/99) reproduce exactly.
- WR>TE 6 → model 6 (exact). TE>DIME 12 → model 12 (exact). TE>FS 12 → model 12 (exact, QB special-cased). QB>FS 12 → model 12 (exact, special-cased). FB>TE 6 → model 6 (exact). WR>TE anchor reproduced.
- The two hard misses are exactly CB>FS (0→6) and CB>SS (0→6), as stated.
- No per-flex-row override table exists; the only special cases are Owner-doctrine ones (FB free destination, QB→FS only, K↔P).

### Not reproduced (anchor-case miss)
- ILB>OLB (Owner "MIKE>WILL free" = 0): model gives tier 2 (raw 2.86) — off-by-one, NOT free. The submission lists this as an Owner key case captured by the model; it is not.
- OLB>ILB (Owner "WILL>MIKE 6"): model gives tier 2 (raw 2.24) — off-by-one, not 6.

Both linebacker↔linebacker directions are off-by-one in this model, which undercuts the "key cases as anchors" claim. Worth a sentence in the design doc (the cross-family/tax calibration does not capture the Owner's linebacker asymmetry).

### Accounting clarifications
- "Per-rule overrides: 0" is accurate for per-flex-row overrides, but `price()` contains 3 Owner-doctrine special cases (FB free destination, QB→FS only at 12, K↔P at 2). Recommend relabelling as "0 per-row overrides (3 Owner-doctrine special cases)".
- "4 hyperparameters" understates the hand-set constants: there are 4 scalars (VALUE_TAX, CROSS_TAX, plus tier thresholds 1/4/9) but also a 14-entry FAM_TAX table and 8-entry duty weight table W, plus hand-authored CAP/DEMAND (19×8 each). The "few, principled" framing should acknowledge the family-tax table and duty weights as additional authored inputs.
- Portability (cross-sport) is INFERRED, not verified: the duty vocabulary and CAP/DEMAND profiles are football-authored; the basketball mapping is asserted. Acceptable as a design claim, but should be labelled INFERRED until a basketball run exists.

### Not checked (no tool / no data)
- The "beautiful/easy to explain in UI" and "zero overrides to maintain" claims are design judgments; not verifiable by command.
- I did not re-derive slot_value from design.json or audit design.json's spot weights.
