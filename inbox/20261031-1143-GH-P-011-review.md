VERDICT: rejected

DEFECT (shown): The seeded draft cannot continue deterministically after a refresh. The work says to save the seed and resume by assigning the saved `draftSim`, but the referenced persistence proposal initializes `_rng` to `Math.random` and its Resume handler never calls `setSeed`. It also does not serialize the PRNG's current state/cursor. After reload, `processUntilUserTurn()` therefore draws from a fresh unseeded RNG; restoring only the original seed would restart the sequence rather than continue it. Checked the work and `_agents/beam/notes/persistence-proposal.md` from `origin/council/beam`, plus the “same seed” and refresh requirements in `origin/council/base:tasks/T5-persistence.md`.

DEFECT (shown): Export does not honor the promised private-mode/storage failure handling. The referenced `exportSave()` calls `localStorage.getItem(SAVE_KEY)` outside a `try/catch`; if storage is unavailable, the preceding `saveNow()` may catch its own error, but export then throws instead of reporting that export is unavailable.

NOT RUN: Browser refresh/export tests. The work is a proposal and no implementation or runnable test harness was supplied.

Weakest point: the proposal claims deterministic seeded drafts while neither restoring nor persisting the PRNG continuation state. A refresh mid-draft can silently change the remaining draft's randomness.
