## Findings

### Conflicts with the Owner's rulings (must be resolved by the Owner, not by the council)

1. **White text (Rule 1 vs Z.ai Commandment 1 vs Owner).** Beam: "White (#f5f0f0) is only for headings and emphasis." Z.ai: "No white text. Anywhere. Ever." The Owner's 24 Oct ruling explicitly says: "White text for names and sentences; shimmering gold / rose-gold for anything exciting." The Owner ALLOWS white text for names and sentences. Both Beam and Z.ai are too restrictive. **The Owner's ruling supersedes both.** The law must state: white text is allowed for names and sentences; gold/rose-gold for emphasis and excitement. Flag as the most important conflict.

2. **Integers only (Rule 2 vs Z.ai Commandment 2).** Beam allows 1 decimal for win probability ("e.g., 62.3%"). Z.ai forbids all decimals ("moment a value reaches the screen it is rounded"). P-156 uses 1 decimal for "How your draft ranks". Conflict. The Owner hasn't ruled explicitly on decimals. Recommend: allow 1 decimal only where the Owner's brief explicitly requests it (e.g., "How your draft ranks" 1 decimal per P-156), and integers everywhere else. Flag for Owner ruling.

3. **Gold colour (Rule 10 vs Z.ai).** Beam/Z.ai use `#d4af37` (dark) / `#b8941f` (light). The Owner's Pre-Colors build uses `#ffc43c` (light gold). The Owner's 24 Oct ruling: "Bright gold stays on dark; light mode needs a deeper gold for small text." Beam's own earlier review said "Light gold (#ffc43c) preferred over dark gold (#d4af37) unless too yellow." Conflict. Recommend: use `#ffc43c` as the canonical gold (matching Pre-Colors) and document the light-mode deeper variant. Flag.

4. **Fade timing (Rule 4).** Beam: fade in 800ms, fade out 400ms. Z.ai: ~4.2s cross-fade. The Owner's 24 Oct ruling doesn't specify timing. Conflict. Recommend: keep Beam's shorter timing (800/400) for UI transitions and Z.ai's longer timing for cinematic title sequences. Or pick one. Flag for Owner ruling.

5. **Team colours scope (Rule 14 vs Z.ai Commandment 3).** Beam: "Player names use warm white, not team colour." Z.ai: "team palette drives everything (scores in team colour, etc.)." The Owner's 24 Oct ruling: "once a team is chosen every accent uses that team's three hexes, except players on opponent teams." So team colours apply to accents, not player names. Beam is closer. But Z.ai's "scores in team colour" is more expansive. The Owner says "players on opponent teams" are excepted — implying player names are not team-coloured. Recommend Beam's version. But flag the scope difference.
