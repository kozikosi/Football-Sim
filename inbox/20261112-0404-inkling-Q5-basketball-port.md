IDEA: basketball breaks the lineup solver, not the formula — the port is cheaper than everyone assumes (minutes ARE exposures; the duty tables are the same shape) except exactly one place: football lineup is an assignment problem (Hungarian), basketball lineup is a composition problem (C(15,5)); if the formula is going to port, that is where the new measurement hides.

## The five inputs, ported one by one (VERIFIED by inspection of the design; numbers INFERRED where noted)

1. **Positions.** Football: 32 slots (formation-remapped). Basketball: 15–17 roster players, 5 on floor, 5–7 rotation — there is no fixed slot table at all; positions are role archetypes (PG/SG/SF/PF/C), and the "position" of the 20-0 engine becomes the *spending* structure, not the slot.
2. **Duties.** Football: 9 channels (RUSH, RDEF, RNG, TKL, MCOV, ZCOV, DEEP, CTH, EREP). Basketball duty table sketch (the HAVE columns about what a lineup must execute): rim protection (BLK/verticality), on-ball containment (matchup pressure), spot-up shooting (3PT at guarded/unguarded), ball handling under pressure (turnover avoidance vs blitz), playmaking (advantage creation), rebounding (contest/box-out), spacing (gravity — defender-distance creation), rim finishing. 8 duties ≈ football's 9, no creativity needed.
3. **HAVE.** Player → duties: shot-making at volume (TS%), assist/usage efficiency (AST%), block rate, steal rate, 3PA rate + C%, limiting-rim-attempt rate, defensive rebound%, turnover%. All public, all per-100 possessions, all in the box score — the "HAVE" for basketball is fully observable, unlike football's tape-room HAVE of RNG/EREP.
4. **NEED.** Lineup needs its five chosen to clear each duty: floor spacing (≥2 shooters), rim protection (≥1 verticality), on-ball containment (+1 rim-pressurer), rebounding (≥1 box-out), + one flex variable (the stretch-5 vs traditional 5). Same "worst deficiency" structure (M), same accumulation (S) — the form M + λS survives intact.
5. **Weights + λ + cuts + floor.** λ again unidentifiable (only β = λ·θ, the freeze normalizes — DS D3 is sport-agnostic). The weights scale with the duty table; λ is sport-noise, all-period, and the dial logic carries.

**The one that does NOT carry over directly, and everything interesting is inside it:**

**Exposure.** Football's E = play share (measured by snap counts). Basketball's exposure is **minutes share** — which public logs already have (36-minute ceiling, 41 for stars). So the exposure factor ports verbatim as a concept: the guard becomes the minutes guard, discounting the price-impact of a part-time body by its minutes. That's the easiest half of the port, and it means **the basketball Phase 1 can be measured on public data without the Owner's ratings** — the way football couldn't (needs snap shares, mapped to Madden OVRs).

## Where the formula actually breaks — the solver, not the price

Football: 32 slots; exactly 1 player per slot; the value is additive; the optimal assignment is the **Hungarian algorithm** — one line of vendored code, exact, fast, done.

Basketball: pick **5 of 15**: 3,003 lineups per game state, times positional mixes, times swapping fatigue/matchups, times two halves ≈ 10⁴–10⁵ states per possession — and the objective is **not additive** (a lineup with two ball-dominant guards produces negative spacing value on top of doubled usage — the same resource contention football prices as usage; carry it into the objective and the lineup joins a non-separable optimization). The Hungarian cannot solve a non-separable lineup objective because there is no square assignment problem inside it.

**The port needs one new thing football never asked for: a lineup-composition solver.** Cheapest honest shape: constrain to the realistic rotation (starters + 2 bench of 18 plausible fives) and exhaust them (3,003 × ~20 credible units = 60k lineups — a laptop-minute), exactly the way football enumerate 5×5 offensive sub-units could work for the two-point-conversion case. The formula (M + λS + max guard + exposure) then prices each five; the captain of the five is not a slot, it is the ATO captain role, priced by the same guard: the scarcest job in basketball is the best ball-handler playing < half the snaps, by Football's own law — the guard exists precisely for full-time roles (the star ball-handler capitalizes rarely, i.e. maximum arbitrage when he is FULL-time — which is most of the time for stars; the part-time arbitrage is the backup PG on a vet team, E ≈ 0.25, the same part-time case as the FB family).

## What the formula needs that football never asked

1. **A contention term** (usage ceiling per lineup): football contention is usage share inside one player; basketball contention is usage share inside the five. Without it, the solver over-prices ball-dominant pairs (own version of the FB exploit). Fit from the logs, not by hand: lineup points/100 vs Σ(ball-dominant players) per lineup — the over-concentration penalty falls out of the same variance-decomposition play DS runs on team-game means; INTERNALLY the same method as the overdispersion filing already written. ~1 column of code, no new data.
2. **A substitution-fatigue clock term** (nothing in football has play-to-play fatigue priced into the lineup), or explicitly out of scope for Phase 1 — INFERRED it stays out. The 20-0 engine can start without it and nobody will see it for five seasons; call it out instead of pretending it away.
