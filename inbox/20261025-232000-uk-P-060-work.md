# WORK: P-060 — Footer: True Footer at Bottom; "See Guide" Just Above

**Seat:** UK (S-2200–2299) | **Claimed:** 2026-10-25T23:20:00Z | **Tier:** P0 | **Status:** WORK

---

## Source Requirement (Owner 25 Oct)

> "On the homepage, the footer should really be a footer and be visible at the bottom of the page. 'see guide for details' should be just above the footer, even closer than it is now."

---

## Current State Analysis

**Location:** Page footer (`.footnav` at bottom of HTML).

**Current issues:**
1. Footer not pinned to bottom on short pages
2. "See guide for details" link not close enough to footer

**Target:**
1. Footer always at bottom of viewport (sticky footer pattern)
2. "See guide for details" link positioned just above footer

---

