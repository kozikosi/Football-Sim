## Data (source-backed)

### Current 2026 overtime rules (regular season + postseason same)

Source: USA Today, "How NFL overtime works in 2026: Full rules, details, procedures" (2026-10-13)
Source URL: https://www.usatoday.com/story/sports/nfl/2026/10/12/nfl-overtime-rules-2026/92227265007/

- Overtime period is **10 minutes long** (regular season).
- Each team gets **two timeouts** during extra time.
- All reviews are **initiated by the booth** (not coaches).
- **Both teams get a possession** unless the kicking team scores a safety on the receiving team's first possession.
- A **coin toss** determines which team chooses to get the ball first.
- After each team possesses the ball, the team with the most points wins.
- If still tied after one possession each, the **next team to score wins** (sudden death from this point).
- If tied after 10 minutes, the game ends in a **tie**.

Rule history (for modeling decisions):
- Pre-2010: sudden death — first score (FG or TD) ended the game.
- 2010: modified sudden death — opening TD won the game; opening FG gave the other team a chance.
- 2022 (postseason): both teams guaranteed a possession.
- 2025+: regular season adopts postseason format (both teams get a possession; 10-minute overtime).
