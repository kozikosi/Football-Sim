--- DESIGN PROPOSAL ---

Concept: A 4-step spotlight overlay that highlights key UI elements on first load. Dismissible, re-showable from settings. No images, no CDN.

Steps:
1. Draft board: "This is your draft board — pick players to fill your roster. Each player shows Wins and Priority."
2. Weights table: "Adjust weights to value positions your way. The default comes from the model."
3. Play season button: "Play the season once your roster is set. Simulate week by week or all at once."
4. Season review: "Review results, awards, and the playoff bracket. Can you go 20-0?"

Visual design:
- Semi-transparent overlay (rgba(0,0,0,0.7)) covering the page.
- Spotlight cutout around the target element (box-shadow: 0 0 0 9999px rgba(0,0,0,0.7)).
- Tooltip card near the target: title + description + "Next" / "Skip" buttons.
- Step indicator: "1 of 4" dots.
- Smooth transition between steps (fade out spotlight, fade in next).
- Dismiss: click "Skip" or press Esc. Re-showable from settings ("Show tour").

Implementation:
- localStorage flag: `tourDone = "1"`.
- On first load (no flag), start tour after 500ms delay (let page render).
- Target elements identified by selector: #draftBoard, #weightsTable, #playSeasonBtn, #seasonReview.
- Position tooltip relative to target (above/below based on viewport).
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion: instant transitions (no fade).
