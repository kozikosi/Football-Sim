### Distance-specific accuracy tiers (nflstats.org)

Five tiers tracked: 50+, 55+, 60+, 65+ yards. Data aggregates 2000-present. Raw success rate and Composite Score available per kicker/team/year.

Source: nflstats.org, "NFL Kicking Dashboard" — league trends section.

### Summary of distance-bracket accuracy
- 20-29 yd: ~90-93% (very high)
- 30-39 yd: ~85-89% (high)
- 40-49 yd: ~70-75% (moderate)
- 50-59 yd: ~58-66% (low-moderate)
- 60+ yd: ~45-60% (very low, ~50% in 60+ tier)
