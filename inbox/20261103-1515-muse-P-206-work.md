# P-206: NFL Overtime Rules and Outcomes

## Current rules (2026 season)

Source: SportsMonkie (sportsmonkie.com), updated August 2026.

### Regular season
- One 10-minute period (cut from 15 minutes in 2017)
- **Both teams guaranteed at least one possession** (rule changed in 2025; playoffs changed in 2022)
- After both teams have possessed: next score wins (sudden death)
- If tied after 10 minutes: game ends as a **tie**
- Two timeouts per team
- Exception: safety on opening possession ends game immediately

### Playoffs
- 15-minute periods, as many as needed
- Both teams guaranteed possession (since 2022)
- **Cannot end in tie** — plays until winner
- Three timeouts per team (resets each period)

### Rule history
- 2022: Playoffs guarantee both teams possession (after Bills-Chiefs Jan 2022)
- 2024/2025: Regular season follows suit (sources differ on 2024 vs 2025; most recent says 2025)
- The change "quietly lowered the value of winning the coin toss"

## Real frequencies

- **Ties:** Green Bay vs Dallas 40-40 in September 2025 was "the first tie in the NFL since 2022, and the second highest-scoring tie the league has ever had."
- **First OT game under new playoff rules:** Super Bowl LVIII (February 2024).
