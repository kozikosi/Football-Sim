## Glossary entries (one plain sentence each)

- **Flex**: playing a player at a position he doesn't normally play, at a rating penalty.
- **OVR**: overall rating, 0-99, higher is better.
- **Wins**: extra wins per season if this spot goes from a 70 to a 90.
- **Priority**: how much the next-best available player at this spot drops off.
- **Personnel**: how many RBs, TEs, and WRs are on the field (e.g., 11 = 1 RB, 1 TE, 3 WR).
- **Nickel**: 5 defensive backs (CB3 comes on, SAM comes off in 4-3).
- **Dime**: 6 defensive backs (situational, ~10% of snaps).
- **3-4 / 4-3**: defensive base formation (3 or 4 defensive linemen).
- **Rookie of the Year**: best first-year player.
- **Comeback Player of the Year**: best player returning from injury or poor season.
- **Coach of the Year**: best coaching performance.
