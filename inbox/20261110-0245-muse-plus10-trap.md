## The trap (Z.ai's finding, verified by MUSE against the §2 table)

Z.ai's w88 verification (§5) found a trap in the localWins20 method. If someone "improves" the method by replacing the slope × 20 with a direct centered 20-span — wins(center+10) − wins(center−10) — they silently re-import the OLD slotWins20 numbers and erase the whole change.

The numbers (Z.ai, verified from the frozen Colors engine at snapshot `4ca9a071`):

| Slot | OLD slotWins20 (70→90) | NEW localWins20 (slope × 20 at ±5) | Direct ±10 span wins(center+10) − wins(center−10) |
|---|---|---|---|
| SAM | 0.2630 | 0.1279 | **0.2630 (identical to OLD)** |
| RB2 | 0.2468 | 0.0971 | **0.2468 (identical to OLD)** |
| TE2 | 0.3702 | 0.2661 | **0.3702 (identical to OLD)** |

## Why it happens (the mechanism)

The slotWins curve is near-linear across a 20-point span. A secant over any 20-point window ≈ the secant over 70→90. So a centered 20-span (center±10) gives back the OLD number, not the NEW one.

The local slope × 20 is different: it measures the rate at the pool mean over a SMALL window (±5, a 10-point span), then scales by 20 to express it as Wins for a 20-point OVR span. That local rate differs from the 20-point secant when the curve has curvature:
- **SAM:** the curve is flat around the pool average (LB mean 72.47) but steeper across 70→90. Local slope 0.128; OLD secant 0.263. The ±10 span averages over the steeper region and returns 0.263.
- **TE2:** the curve is strongly convex (curving upward). Local slope at ±5 is 0.266; the 20-point secant is 0.370. The ±10 span averages over the convex region and returns 0.370.
- **RB2:** same pattern (local 0.097, OLD secant 0.247, ±10 span returns 0.247).

## The solution (our position)

**Keep slope × 20 at ±5. Do not use a centered 20-span.**

The ±5 slope is what actually measures the local rate at the pool mean. The ±10 span is not a "better" version of the same measurement — it is a different measurement (the average rate over a wide span), and for these near-linear curves it happens to equal the OLD secant. Using it would silently undo the P-321 change for the three slots that moved most (SAM, RB2, TE2).

This answers P-326's question (4): "Is slope × 20 the right Wins, or should it be wins(center+10) − wins(center−10)?" **Slope × 20 is right. The centered span is the trap.** Do not use it.
