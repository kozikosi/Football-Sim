## Part 7 — is "look like w89" still the right bar? (lighter)

**Answer one: with w89 the shell cannot "meet" any beauty bar by accident — w89 is a single-file wave from an era before inlined fonts and the engine's Year-One (the zpb--17 codes). The shell exists to be judged as the product (offline, single-file, install-and-play 76 Pancakes), and w89 should be the *palette* source (light + dark + team takeover tokens) and the *voice/structure* source (the mythic 20-0 jingle, the gold-family family, the honest plain-language recaps the engine already speaks), not a proof-target for layout. A beauty bar the Owner can sign as "pass" in one screenshot does not exist on "look exactly like w89" terms without reversion of real improvements; the right bar is: *same palette physics + same promise + same restraint; different era.* If the Owner wants literal parity, the seat-select sheet can do every item in Part 6 within a sitting — but the decision should be made on intent, not on drift.

**Answer two: the takeover shouldn't be "every token everywhere" — it's too broad for signal and creates the same infinite contrast work again. The right design: a minimal set of five tokens carries team identity — `--accent` (primary team colour), `--accent-ink` (accessible text on panels — computed), `--gold-accent` (the team's accentified gold — i.e. re-mix the golds toward team hue), `--panel-tint` (barely-tinted panel), `--team-hairline` — applied to: panels, borders/hairlines, gold pills, the on-clock line, the wordmark track, and the "you" glow in the feed. Keep `--zcream`/light neutrals and the *info-vs-chrome distinction* team-static deliberately — that's the whole difference between "gold for everyone" and "this is MY team". w89's "every accent token" approach is the maximalist version of the same idea and works — at the cost of every row in every sheet needing a re-audit; the five-token set deletes 90% of the re-audit burden while preserving the felt difference.
