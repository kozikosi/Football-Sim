## Design issue (the reason for "improved")

4. **The blend form is weaker than max + β·min.** VL v3 proposes "a weighted α·A + (1−α)·G" as the malleable dial, with max(A,G) and A+G sum as the "two measured points." But a weighted average does NOT include max or sum as settings — it ranges from G (α=0) to A (α=1), passing through the average, and it can go BELOW max(A,G). That matters: for C>OT (A = 2.03, G = 14.00), the weighted average at α = 0.5 is 0.5×2.03 + 0.5×14.00 = **8.0 → tier 6, WRONG** (owner 12). The blend only keeps C>OT at 12 for α ≤ ~0.3 (mostly guard) — a narrow, skewed useful range. Compare the max + β·min form (MUSE-D2, Inkling-Q1): at β = 0 it is pure max (14.0 → 12 ✓), at β = 0.5 it is 14.0 + 0.5×2.03 = 15.0 → 12 ✓, at β = 1.0 it is the sum (16.0 → 12 ✓). The max + β·min form keeps C>OT at 12 for ALL β and includes max (β=0) and sum (β=1) as endpoints. VL's weighted average does neither. **Recommendation: adopt the max + β·min blend (already measured by MUSE-D2 and Inkling-Q1) instead of the weighted average.** The "adjustable ratio" the Owner asked for (Q7) is better served by a dial whose endpoints are the two measured rulings and whose floor is the worse ingredient.
