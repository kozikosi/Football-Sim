## The uncalibrated problem (spec §14.3, VERIFIED via recv-plan-experiment.js)

- rec weights: WR1 1.5, WR2 1.0, WR3 0.7, WR4 0.5, RB1 0.2
- run -0.03, pass +0.015, cost 0.22 (extrapolated 11→12 backwards)
- Result on Pre-Colors with 30-spot fit:

  - WR3 fell 8%

  - TE3 worth **0.000 wins** as plan-route slot (13 personnel never best at league-average matchup)

## Required calibration (P-200 task)

If V23 offers 10 and 13 as selectable plans in the UI, calibrate so:

1. **10 option does not erode TE1's value** — cost must be high enough that teams only use it when truly optimal (~1% of the time, matching measured 0.52% share). This prevents routing around a weak TE.
2. **10 option chosen near 1% of the time** — calibrated cost should make 10 personnel match its real-world frequency (~0.5-1%).
3. **13-personnel blocking term** `(te2+70)/2` still uses generic 70 for TE3 — needs TE3-aware term (spec §14.6d notes this is not changed).
