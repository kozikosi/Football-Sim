VERDICT: rejected

The proposal captures several real Owner preferences, but it does not meet P-183's requested testable style-law format and its “VERIFIED” claim is too strong: it contains internal contradictions and rules that conflict with, or go beyond, the cited Owner rulings.

- **Defect (shown):** rule 1 says “NO WHITE TEXT,” then assigns `#f5f0f0` as warm-white body text and permits white headings/emphasis. This directly conflicts with the later Owner ruling recorded in the intake ledger: no white text; neutral text must be a pale tint of the palette. Z.ai's cited style guide also explicitly forbids near-white defaults.
- **Defect (shown):** rule 14 confines selected-team colors to “team-specific elements,” but the Owner's request says team colors transform the app and every accent after team selection, with only the stated tab/player exceptions. This rule would suppress the requested app-wide theming.
- **Defect (shown):** rule 15 is self-contradictory: its heading says “NO PERMANENT GREEN,” while its body says green “must not appear anywhere.” The Owner's concern is permanent/default green; a global ban would also conflict with using official team colors for green teams.
- **Defect (shown):** rules 5 and 8 conflict on exact Owner wording. Rule 5 would capitalize the one-word button as `UNDO`, while the cited Owner label is `↶ Undo`; the rule must preserve explicit Owner text rather than apply a blanket casing transformation.
- **Defect (shown):** P-183 asks for every rule to be a testable check with a selector or regex and expected count. The proposal is only prose: it provides no selectors, regexes, counts, or equivalent executable acceptance checks.
- **Calibration question:** rule 2 grants one decimal to win probability, but the cited N1 exception is one decimal for the “How your draft ranks” position only. The broad win-probability exception is not sourced in this proposal.
- **Calibration question:** rule 4 chooses 800ms fade-in and 400ms fade-out, although the cited style guide describes a roughly 4.2-second fade cycle and the Owner asks for slow fades. Provide evidence for those timings or leave durations unresolved rather than labeling them Owner-verified.
- **Calibration question:** shimmer threshold varies in Owner inputs: the 24 Oct request describes 85+ as foil shimmer, while later notes request shimmer at 90+ and a more vibrant treatment at 85+. The proposal adopts 90+ without recording that conflict; the brief says to flag conflicts rather than choose.
- **Risk:** rule 16's “audio toggle persists” requirement is not sourced in the cited Owner notes or style guide. Rule 11's global no-emoji ban also broadens specific Owner requests to remove emojis from badges; distinguish an adopted Owner rule from an unsourced proposal.
- **Weakest point:** this is presented as a verified canonical law, but important rules contradict one another or direct Owner instructions, and none of the requested checks are testable as written.
