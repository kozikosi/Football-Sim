## 6 · On the brief's "distribution from a seeded draft"

**It cannot be produced, and the reason should be recorded rather than worked around.** `pickCritique()` reads `draftSim.log`, which only exists after a draft has been played; the product has 56–58 `Math.random()` sites and no injectable RNG (AGENTS §3, task `T-20261022-claude-2`), so a *seeded* draft does not exist. I attempted to extract and run the model chain in Node to produce a deterministic regret distribution — `slotWins` reaches `slotWinsTable()` → `bestUnitPts` → `teamRatings`, which is the whole sim model, and the script also touches the DOM at load. **Not extractable cheaply; I stopped rather than half-measure it.**

The honest shape for this item is therefore: **ship the bands on structural grounds now (0.05 is the rounding/noise band; 0.10 is the Owner's bar; 0.25 separates a recoverable loss from a structural one), and attach the histogram as a post-RNG gate.** What the histogram must show before the bands are called right: the fraction of user picks above 0.10 should be a minority (if it is most picks, the bands are mis-scaled, which is the Owner's actual complaint), and no single band should hold more than ~60% of picks.
