## Why "improved" rather than "ok"

1. **Palette violates the theme north star.** The prototype ships a Kansas-City red/gold/white palette (`--kc-red:#E31837`, `--kc-gold:#FFB612`, `--bg:#FDF7F7`). The Owner's rulings: **rose until a team is chosen**; Home/Season/Playoffs stay rose; team colours only transform the app *after* the user picks a team and starts the draft. The homepage is pre-selection, so it must be rose-gold-white, not KC red. Using a chosen team's colours on the homepage also conflicts with "each player's draft card keeps the colours of their real NFL team until the draft ends" (team theming is post-pick). → default to Beam's rose palette (`--bg` barely-pink rose, gold `#d4af37`-ish, white), and make the marquee/home button/gold gradient rose-gold, not red-gold.
2. **Gold drifts orange.** `#FFB612` (KC gold) is an orange-leaning gold; the Owner explicitly warns "gold must not drift orange, e.g. behind 'Super Bowl Champion!'". Use a neutral gold and, per the contrast ruling, a **deeper gold for small text in light mode** (Beam's contrast table: light gold `#b8941f` on `#fdf5f7` = 2.68:1 FAIL; `#8f6a0e` = 4.62:1 AA). VL's floaty text `1.35–1.7rem` in `#FFB612` on `#FDF7F7` will fail AA. → contrast table required (P-003 owns the tokens; P-001 must consume them, not redefine `--kc-*`).
3. **"No grey/black anywhere" is breached.** `--ink:#1A1718` (near-black) and `--muted:#8D6E63` (brown). The ruling: "Neutral = white; no black or grey except dark mode's black." Body text in light mode may be dark rose, but near-black ink and a brown muted token are not in the rose/gold/white system. → replace with rose-deep ink and a rose muted token; keep any dark text only where contrast requires it, documented.
4. **"Other text" remains on the home page.** The Owner removed the tagline, stepper, "Set Up Your Draft", the "Season, Playoffs, Super Bowl…" line, and all other text; only the guide fades in on scroll. The prototype still has: four floaty text entities (`20-0`, `KC`, `SF`, `SUPER BOWL`), a "How to draft" guide card with a paragraph, and a bottom `.note` paragraph. The floats and the note are "other text" and must go (or be re-classified as decoration with an explicit Owner decision). The guide card content should be the *only* text that fades in, and it should fade in *from nothing* (no initial flash).
5. **Select placeholders are label text.** `<option>Draft as</option>` and `<option>Draft pick #</option>` are exactly the "Draft as / Draft pick" label text the Owner removed. They must be empty/aria-labelled floating inputs (e.g. `<option value="" disabled selected hidden></option>` + `aria-label="Draft as"`), not visible placeholder options.
6. **DRAFT button font.** Owner: "New font for the main DRAFT button." The prototype uses the system font for `.startdraft` (only the label clip changes). → specify a distinct font stack for the button and show the receipt.
7. **Size limit.** 10435 B > 10240 B. Trim (remove the trailing `.note` block, collapse whitespace, or move the delivery to `deliveries/` with the size noted). The inbox claim of "~4 KB" is wrong and must be corrected.
