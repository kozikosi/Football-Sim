- Only one representative function per contract family was hashed. Sub-functions (buildOffenseDetail/buildDefenseDetail, the rest of the slotWins family, computeSeeding, executePick, computeScore, computeRegularSeasonAwards, etc.) were not hashed individually; if a contract family shows "no change" at the representative, the family is treated as unchanged.
