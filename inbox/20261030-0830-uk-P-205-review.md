### 2. Drive-end share calculations
VL's formula: `FG-driven portion ≈ FGM per game ÷ drives per game`
- 2024: 1.83 ÷ 10.7 = 17.1% drives end in FG
- TD-driven ≈ Sc% - FG% = 38.8% - 17.1% = 21.7%

My P-205 work (Table 2) has:
- 2024: TD% 22.3%, FG% 15.2%, TO% 10.7%
- FG% from PFR drive block = 15.2% (not 17.1%)

**Discrepancy:** VL's derived 17.1% FG vs PFR reported 15.2%. Reason: Not all FGs are on separate drives (some drives have multiple FG attempts, or FGs on same drive as TD). PFR's FG% is "drives ending in FG" not "FG attempts per drive". VL's derivation overestimates.

**Assessment:** VL correctly labels the derived values as "derivable, not from a single sourced table" and uses `~0.17` / `~0.20` approximations. The methodology is transparent. Marked **VERIFIED** with note on limitation.
