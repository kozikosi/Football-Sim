## Verdict

**Inkling's design is stronger and should be the primary design.** It reproduces the Owner's table more accurately (97/105 exact vs Super's 4 anchor disagreements), it handles the FS case (the hardest part of item 3), it accounts for the WILL/C bump, and it correctly identifies the Owner overrides (C>OG, FB caps, the five U rows) as the smallest such set. Super's design is simpler and cleaner, but it fails on the FS case, the WILL→MIKE case, the WR→CB case, the EDGE→OLB case, and the OG→C case — five disagreements that all require convention overrides to fix, which erodes the simplicity advantage.

**Super's design is not wrong — it is a useful simplification.** The 7-skill matrix is cleaner than Inkling's 9-skill vector, and Super's "profile computes a base price, then Owner conventions override" principle (§6) is the right architecture. The two designs should be combined: take Inkling's accuracy (the D-gate, the COV dmults, the bump) and Super's clarity (the 7-skill matrix, the convention-override architecture).

## What must change before the Architect builds

1. **Inkling's 97/105 figure needs independent verification.** The scratch script (`p322final2.py`) was not committed. Someone (a seat, or the Architect on build) must re-run the fit against the final 105-rule table and confirm 97/105. If the figure does not reproduce, the design's accuracy claim weakens.
2. **The gate needs a decision.** Inkling's gate (D ≥ 5 → A counts 0) is a simplification with a visible seam. The Architect should either keep it with named constants (GATE_LO=4, GATE_HI=5) or replace it by re-deriving D. This is an Architect decision, not a council one.
3. **Super's SNAP problem needs a fix.** If Super's 7-skill matrix is used, SNAP must be re-weighted (0.5) or treated as a bonus, or OG→C will price at 12 against the Owner's 2. This is a design defect, not a disagreement.
4. **Both designs need P-323 to land first.** The items-3+7 table (105 rules) has not landed on the Architect's branch. The skill profile should be fit against the 105-rule table, not the 97-rule table. P-323 is the gate.

## Recommendation to the Architect

Take Inkling's design as the primary (it is the most accurate), adopt Super's convention-override architecture (profile + small override list), and resolve the gate + SNAP questions on build. Do not build either design silently re-pricing protected constants — FLEX is protected; any build re-pins the hash in MERGE-MANIFEST.md in the same commit.
