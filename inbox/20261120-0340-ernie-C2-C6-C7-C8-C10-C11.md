## C2 · Review of the passGap study as a decision document

**VERDICT: agree with the decision** (product stays λ=0, GLM off; the open item is the ratings→gap mapping). The numbers are verified twice; this is about whether the conclusion is the right one.

1. **λ=0 is right, and the reason is stronger than "the mapping is unmeasured".** The study measured a tilt in *r units* — a residual score the product cannot produce from Madden ratings without a mapping that has never been fit. Turning λ on today would apply a *guessed* gap to a real distribution, which is worse than off: it would move points by an unknown amount with no way to attribute the change. Keep it off until `gap = f(ratings)` exists and has been checked against the measured spread.
2. **The fit spec is testable, but it needs four conditions, not three.** As written ("match mean 0, sd ≈ 0.024, and 0.45/0.2 persistence") it is a direction, not a test. Make it:
   - (a) the ratings-based gap has **mean ≈ 0** per season;
   - (b) its **sd is within ±20% of 0.024** (the measured combined gap);
   - (c) its **year-to-year correlation is ≥ 0.3 offense / ≥ 0.1 defense** (the measured 0.45/0.2, with margin);
   - (d) the induced `s = λ·gap` keeps points inside **±4% at |s| = 0.25** (the item-6 scale).
   Falsified if (b) is off by more than ~2×, or (c) is below the measured values.
3. **Add the sign convention.** A positive gap must mean the same thing in every sport and every surface (for football: favours the offence). The report never states it, and the mapping's sign is a free parameter — a sign error would move points the wrong way and still pass (a).
4. **One risk the report does not name: the target is a team-season mean, but the sim needs a game-level gap.** Matching the *mean* and the *sd* of team-season tilt scores does not guarantee the week-to-week variation is right. The fit should also match the **game-level** distribution (or at least the within-season variance), or the sim will have the right average tilt with the wrong week-to-week texture.
5. **The θ caveat is sufficient if one sentence is added:** "λ is quoted for θ = [0.3, −0.2, −1.0, −0.6]; a different θ rescales it as 1/c²." The report's current wording is close but does not say the number is *conditional* on the vector.

## C8 · Third-seat check on the Q7 test

**VERDICT: the law is pinned for form/focus/flags; one gap.**

The test (`colors/test/nfl/valueprovider.test.ts`, the first new test) builds:

```ts
const neutral = neutralSnapshot(2026, 5, 'sha256:p', 'e1');
const loaded  = makeSnapshot({ season: 2026, week: 5, packHash: 'sha256:p', engineVersion: 'e1', form: {…}, focus: {…}, flags: {…} });
```

It then asserts equality for every provider × base × slot. That catches a provider that reads `form`, `focus` or `flags` — the three Q7 context fields. **It does not catch a provider that reads `snapshot.week` or `snapshot.season`**, because those are identical in the two snapshots. A future "scout view" that priced by week would pass the test while violating the spirit of Q7 (displayed numbers must not move in-season).

**One-line fix:** make the loaded snapshot differ in week (`week: 6`) — or state the scope in the test name ("…for the three context fields"). The former is better: it costs nothing and closes the hole.

## C11 · The standing review protocol for a cakepanz zip

When Z.ai's zip lands, three different seats run:

1. **Snapshot byte-identical to the pin.** `git diff --stat` the engine files against the pin (name the pin in the review); any change to `src/engine/**` is a reject unless the pin moved with a re-pin receipt.
2. **Build clean.** `npm install && npm run build` — exit 0, no warnings that matter.
3. **Battery green.** `npm run battery` — report the pass/fail/skip counts; a SKIP must carry its reason.
4. **390 px.** No horizontal scroll; the new laws hold at both viewports.
5. **Zero console errors.**
6. **The "can it die" test.** For each new law, remove the change in a scratch copy and confirm the law **fails**. A law that passes with and without the change is decoration.
7. **The report matches the artifacts.** Every number in the report must be reproducible from the zip's commands.

**Verdicts:** `ok` (ship as is) · `improved` (ship with the listed fixes) · `rejected` (a snapshot change, a red build, a law that cannot die, or a report that does not reproduce). A rejection needs the evidence, not a vote; a rejection reopens the item.
