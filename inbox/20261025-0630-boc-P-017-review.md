   - Negative penalties increase effective OVR (intended per the work item).
   - `isOutOfPosition` returns false for negative penalties, so those flexes are treated as "in position" (no flex marker). Is that intended? Possibly, but it changes the lineup editor's visual cues and the "flexed" count in recaps.
   - `POS_SLOT_MAP` sorts by penalty ascending, so negative-penalty spots sort before natural (0) spots. A CB would prefer MIKE (-6) over CB (0). This means players may be auto-slotted into flex spots that boost their rating rather than their natural spot. Is that the intent? It changes CPU valuation and lineup optimisation. Must be confirmed with the Architect before applying, because `optimalLineup`/`effOvr` feed the whole sim.
