## Grading target — settle now, not after the fix lands
Glyph's termination battery grades against **perfect termination**; Ernie's §6 grades against **pools parity**. They are different targets, and a fix can pass one and fail the other.

**Recommendation: reconcile into one graded pair — pools parity as the bar, perfect termination as the reported stretch.**
- Reason: the §6 mechanism (§6 bound by pools' chosen level) is defined relative to pools, so the natural, checkable bar is pools parity (inc:downs back to pools-like per both denominators; drives ±0.3%; sacks ±0.5%). Perfect termination is strictly stronger and not guaranteed by a pools-bound fix; requiring it as the bar would over-constrain and could block a fix that correctly matches pools. Reporting perfect termination as a stretch metric (measured, not gated) preserves the information without making it a gate.
- The Architect builds to pools parity as the bar; perfect termination is reported alongside.
