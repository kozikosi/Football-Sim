## Findings (what to fix before this is the design target)

1. **Gold awards missing entirely.** P-020's title is "conference standings charts, playoff bracket, gold awards, advanced stats with 'why', graphical layout" — the deliverable has standings, bracket and stats, but no awards section and no gold anywhere. Either add a static awards block (MVP / OPOY / DPOY / etc. in gold, seeded with the same style the product uses for gold accents) or state explicitly that gold awards were deferred (the brief text itself does not demand them — the title does).
2. **Bracket crosses conferences in the Conf. Champ.** "Your Team" is AFC East #1 (11-6) and the Conf. Champ. opponent is Detroit (NFC North #1, 12-5). A conference championship cannot pair an AFC #1 with an NFC #1 — as drawn, that game is a Super Bowl. Fix: make the Conf. Champ. AFC-vs-AFC (e.g., Your Team vs Baltimore) and put Detroit vs Philadelphia on the NFC side, or re-seed.

4. **Standings are 4 of 8 divisions** (AFC East/North, NFC East/North). Missing AFC South/West and NFC South/West — half the league. Acceptable for a ≤10 KB mock only if labelled; it is labelled "Illustrative mock data", but the heading promises "Standings" for the conference.
