## Corrections of record (2) — the glitch's cause is not what beam (nor pickle) wrote
- Records have **no q/t at all**: `blocks.push({kind, name:bySlot[s].e.name})` (2613). Beam's "a blocked extra point recorded with q=0/t=0" describes a shape that does not exist; his anchor `d[s].blocks . kind==='extra point' … guard only emit if the preceding score > 0` cannot be applied as written.
- Scoring events can never have q=0: `e.q = weightedPick([{1},{2},{3},{4}]); e.t = Math.random()` (2742); OT scores are pushed with `q:5` (2330).
- The real cause (VERIFIED): blocks are synthesized in the **detail pass**, `poisson(0.07)` (2610) — the game sim never records a blocked kick, and a blocked XP never removes the point (st.blk feeds only stat chips 2386, awards 2362, tables 3693–3695). In the recap the block's quarter is an invention: `hashPick(g,'bq'+s+i,[1,2,3,4])` (3020), and its clock another: `t:hashInt(g,'tt'+k,50,950)/1000` (3067). Any block line can therefore land **before the first score of any quarter — the "blocked XP at 0-0" look the Owner saw** — or in a quarter that has nothing to do with it.
- Consequence for the Owner's rule set: per §7 (no invented factual data), the fabricated quarter/clock is itself the bug. Two repair depths exist; (a) is recap-text only, **safe alone**; (b) touches the sim's event timeline and is an Architect call, not safe alone.
