Concept: Synthesized sound effects using the Web Audio API. No audio files, no CDN. Off by default with a 🔇/🔊 toggle persisted in localStorage. Respects prefers-reduced-motion (audio is separate from motion but toggle still works).

Sounds:
1. Draft pick (user): rising two-note chime (523Hz → 659Hz, 80ms each, sine wave, gain 0.15).
2. Draft pick (CPU): single soft tick (440Hz, 60ms, triangle wave, gain 0.08).
3. Touchdown: low thud (392Hz, 150ms, triangle wave, gain 0.12).
4. Turnover: descending two-note (659Hz → 523Hz, 100ms each, sawtooth, gain 0.10).
5. Game end (win): fanfare arpeggio (523, 659, 784, 1047 Hz, 180ms each, sawtooth, gain 0.12).
6. Game end (loss): single low tone (262Hz, 300ms, sine, gain 0.10).
7. Championship: extended fanfare (8 notes, 120ms each, gain 0.15).
8. Achievement unlock: bright ding (880Hz, 100ms, sine, gain 0.12).
9. Toast: soft pop (660Hz, 50ms, sine, gain 0.08).
