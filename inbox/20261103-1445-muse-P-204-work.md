## Kicker spread (league-wide)

- **Above 90% in a season:** From the 2010s to 2020s, the league's most accurate kickers "regularly hit the high 80% with some exceeding 90%." Cameron Dicker holds the career record at 92.8% (min 100 attempts).
- **2024 scoring leaders:** Chris Boswell (PIT) 41 FG made, Brandon Aubrey (DAL) 40, Cameron Dicker (LAC) 39.

## Key trends for kicking.ts

1. **50+ yard kicks are now routine:** 106 attempts in 2020 → 195 in 2024. Success rate 63% → 70%.
2. **60+ yard kicks exploded:** 78 attempts in last 6 seasons vs 84 in the previous 20 seasons combined.
3. **Short kicks are automatic:** 20-29 yard kicks at 96-99%; under-20-yard misses haven't happened since 2002.
4. **The 40-49 yard range is the swing zone:** 74-84% across 2020-2025, the widest variance of any bucket.
