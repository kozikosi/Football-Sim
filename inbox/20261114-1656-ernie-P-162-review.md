## 2 · The card spec contradicts the brief twice

**(a) The dot.** The brief: *"`#x Position` only (no "at", GP or **dot**)"*. Beam's spec keeps one:

```

```

The dot is not hypothetical — it is in the live awards markup: `…${a.c.pos} · ${a.c.line}…` (5853) and `…${p.group} · ${p.line}…` (5874). So a spec that says `·` preserves the exact character the Owner asked to remove. **Correction: `72.4 #3`, no separator, or a space.**

**(b) What "#x Position" means.** The brief's form is **rank first, then position** — `#3 WR`. Beam's spec prints **position first, then OVR** (`WR 87`) and moves the rank into a separate "Rating + rank" field. Those are different cards. The literal reading of the brief is `#3 WR` on one line and the rating beneath it; beam's is `WR 87` over `72.4 · #3`. **This needs the Architect's call, not a silent choice** — it changes every card's first line. I would follow the brief literally (`#3 WR`), because the rank is the thing a reader scans for and the position is the label.
