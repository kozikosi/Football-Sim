## 2. Design shape (aligned to every Q&A answer)

price(src,dst) = clamp( round( combine(A, G) + grav ), 0, cap )

- **A = ability shortfall**: can this body do that job? Duties = what the job demands (pass-block, coverage, snap, rush, catch, throw; basketball: rim protection, ball handling, ...). Authored once, shared with lineup value + flex price (Q5 multisport).
- **G = value guard**: can a cheap player profit by moving up? G = 20 × (1 − V_src / V_dst), G = 0 when V_src ≥ V_dst. V = engine slot value = slotWins20/20 (P-321 measurement). Fully portable, moves automatically when slot values change (P-318); no hand-set weights, no salary table. C>OT = 20×(1−200/500) = 12 natively — Q11(a) value ladder, not an exit premium.
- **combine(A,G)**: adjustable blend per Q7 ("an adjustable ratio... most malleable... easy to tune"). Two measured points: max(A,G) (Inkling, wins under requirements ability) and A+G sum (Claude, wins under his craft layer). Default unset until engine output seen (follow-up Q2). A weighted α·A+(1−α)·G is the malleable dial.
- **grav**: gravity term (Q8, P-335, follow-up Q3: "Now. it might be part of the formula itself"). Placed on G — gravity is value/disruption, not ability. A high-gravity player is more disruptive to move out of; a high-gravity destination harder to fill. Specified as a hook; no numeric term (no gravity data exists yet). Measurement = formal stat (double teams drawn, usage, deterrence = shots/passes not attempted), shown in advanced stats (Q8).
- **cap**: engine-natural max, deferred until engine output seen (follow-up Q2). User sees a simple integer (Q4), no labels ("Free/Slight/Real/Heavy" out), 0/2/6/12 not necessary.
