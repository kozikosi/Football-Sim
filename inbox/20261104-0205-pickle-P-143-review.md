**1. DEFECT (new) — an invented statistic in the proposed glossary: `Dime … (situational, ~10% of snaps)`.**
`AGENTS.md` §7: *"no factual data is invented, estimated or silently 'corrected'"*, and a synthetic or estimated figure may be used **only** where the Owner authorises that specific approach and is labelled as such everywhere. `~10% of snaps` has no source and nothing in the build supports it: the engine models personnel packages and scheme terms, not snap shares (`grep -ci snap` → 7 hits, every one prose or a scheme coefficient, none a dime share — e.g. line 2110 "volume … how many snaps go to each side of the ball"). Delete the number; "Dime: six defensive backs, used in obvious passing situations" is true without it.

**2. DEFECT (new) — the glossary Nickel entry breaks D-012 by giving only the 4-3 case.**
`COUNCIL-BULLETIN.md` D-012 (live ruling): *"Nickel is base-dependent: 4-3 loses SAM (LB3); 3-4 loses the nose tackle (DT1) and keeps all four linebackers. No document may write 'LB3/SAM comes off' without naming the base."* Beam's entry says *"CB3 comes on, SAM comes off in 4-3"* — it names the base, so the letter of D-012 is met, but the Guide is the one place a user learns the rule, and it now teaches half of it. Add the 3-4 half: *"in a 3-4 the nose tackle (DT1) comes off instead and all four linebackers stay"*.
