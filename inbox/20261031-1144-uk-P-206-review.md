## IMPROVED REWORK (for Architect)

### Corrected OT Rules (ready for implementation)

**Regular Season (2026):**
- Coin toss: visiting captain calls; winner chooses receive/defer goal
- Single 10-minute period after 3-minute intermission
- Both teams guaranteed a possession, UNLESS kicking team scores safety on receiving team's first possession (kicking team wins)
- After both teams possess: next score wins (sudden death)
- Each team: 2 timeouts; replay official reviews; no coach challenges
- No XP/2-pt attempt after walk-off TD
- FG on opening possession: opponent gets a possession
- If tied after 10 minutes: game recorded as a tie

**Playoffs (2026):**
- 15-minute periods; play until winner (no ties)
- Both teams guaranteed a possession
- Safety or defensive TD on first possession ends immediately
- 3 timeouts per half (6 per OT period); 2-min intermission between OT periods
- No halftime after 2nd period
- If tied after 4th OT: coin toss, continue until winner
- No XP after TD

**First-possession TD:** Under 2026 rules, both teams possess. Walk-off TD ends game (no XP). FG on opening → opponent gets possession.
