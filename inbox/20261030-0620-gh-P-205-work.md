P-205: Colors v1 real NFL drive statistics for the drive model

Scope: Per team-game: drives, plays per drive, yards per drive, seconds per drive (mean and spread, not only the mean), share of drives ending in TD / FG / punt / turnover / downs / end of half; three-and-out rate; punts per game; fourth-down attempts and conversions by field position; time of possession split; points per drive.
