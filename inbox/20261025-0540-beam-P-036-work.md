--- 10 CANDIDATE STATS ---

1. Flex Tax (Rating Points Lost to Flex)
   Formula: sum(flexPenaltyFor(player.pos, slot) for each starter playing out of position)
   Explanation: "Your lineup lost X rating points to players playing out of position. Move them to their natural spot to recover the points."
   Source: effOvr(), flexPenaltyFor() (l.973-977). Already computed in lineup editor (l.3777).
   Display: Game recap + season review. Red badge if > 0.

2. Roster Balance Score (Standard Deviation of OVR)
   Formula: stddev([ovr for each starter slot])
   Explanation: "Your roster's OVR spread is X. Lower = more balanced; high = top-heavy (stars + scrubs)."
   Source: slotmap OVRs (already in TEAM_DATA). Computable in buildControls().
   Display: Team Comparison + Draft recap.

3. Draft Value Captured
   Formula: sum(playerWar(p).war for each drafted player) - sum(replacementLevel for each slot)
   Explanation: "Your draft captured X wins above replacement. Higher = better value per pick."
   Source: playerWar() (l.~1600), replacementLevels() (l.1355). Already in draft board (Priority column).
   Display: Draft recap + 100-season report.

4. Pass Protection Index
   Formula: avg(OVR of OT1, OT2, OG1, OG2, C)
   Explanation: "Your offensive line's pass protection rating is X. Above 85 = elite; below 75 = vulnerable."
   Source: TEAM_DATA[slot].ovr. Computable from roster.
   Display: Game preview + matchup cards.

5. Defensive Pressure Index
   Formula: avg(OVR of DE1, DE2, DT1, DT2)
   Explanation: "Your defensive line generates pressure at rating X. Above 85 = disruptive; below 75 = passive."
   Source: TEAM_DATA[slot].ovr.
   Display: Game preview + matchup cards.

6. Coverage Index
   Formula: avg(OVR of CB1, CB2, CB3, CB4, FS, SS)
   Explanation: "Your secondary's coverage rating is X. Above 85 = lockdown; below 75 = exploitable."
   Source: TEAM_DATA[slot].ovr.
   Display: Game preview + matchup cards.

7. Turnover Margin
   Formula: (interceptions + fumbles recovered) - (interceptions thrown + fumbles lost)
   Explanation: "Your turnover margin is +X. Positive = you're taking the ball away more than giving it up."
   Source: game stats (already tracked in accumulatePlayerStats). Already in standings.
   Display: Game recap + season review + standings.

8. Sack Rate (per pass attempt)
   Formula: sacks / (pass attempts + sacks) * 100
   Explanation: "Your QB is sacked on X% of dropbacks. Above 8% = protection problem; below 4% = elite."
   Source: game stats (sacks, pass attempts). Already tracked.
   Display: Game recap + advanced stats.

9. Strength of Schedule (avg opponent record)
   Formula: avg(opponent win% for all 17 games)
   Explanation: "Your schedule strength is X (avg opponent win%). Higher = tougher road."
   Source: schedule (buildFullSchedule) + standings (wins/losses). Computable after schedule generation.
   Display: Season review + 100-season report.

10. Red Zone Efficiency
    Formula: (TDs in red zone) / (red zone trips) * 100
    Explanation: "You score a TD on X% of red zone trips. Above 60% = elite; below 40% = need work."
    Source: makeScoringEvents (already tracks scoring by field position). May need a small addition to track red zone trips.
    Display: Game recap + season review.

--- "Why you did well / poorly" explanations ---

Each stat should be accompanied by a one-line explanation in the recap:
- Flex Tax > 0: "You lost X rating points to players out of position. Move [Player] from [Slot] to [Natural Slot] to recover Y points."
- Roster Balance Score > 12: "Your roster is top-heavy. Consider upgrading your depth (RB2, WR3, TE2) for more balance."
- Draft Value Captured < 0: "Your draft captured negative value. You reached on [Player] in round X; [Better Player] was available."
- Pass Protection Index < 75: "Your offensive line can't protect the QB. Sacks and interceptions will follow."
- Defensive Pressure Index < 75: "Your pass rush is weak. Opposing QBs will have all day to throw."
- Coverage Index < 75: "Your secondary is exploitable. Expect big plays against you."
- Turnover Margin < -3: "You're giving the ball away. Protect the ball to win more games."
- Sack Rate > 8%: "Your QB is under siege. Upgrade the offensive line."
- Strength of Schedule > .550: "You faced a tough schedule. Your record may understate your quality."
- Red Zone Efficiency < 40%: "You're settling for field goals. Improve your red zone execution."
