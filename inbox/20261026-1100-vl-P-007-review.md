## What breaks (trying to break it)

1. **Missing playGame side effects (critical).** `animateGame` calls `simulateGame`+`gameFlow`+`buildGameDetail` directly and the integration replaces `playGame(g, isPlayoff)` with `animateGame(g, isPlayoff)`. But `animateGame` as spec'd never calls `playGame`, so it skips: `accumulatePlayerStats(home)`, `accumulatePlayerStats(away)`, the `runDir` tracking block, `g.played = true`, and `seasonState.ratingsDirty = true`. User games would never accumulate player stats or mark as played. Fix: `animateGame` must replicate playGame's full body (or call playGame after computing the result).

2. **Timing math won't reach 30–60 s.** Events are scoring-only (TD/FG/etc.), ~8–12 per game. At 0.8 s each that's ~6–10 s of content. Add kickoff 1 s + 4 quarter breaks (4 s) + halftime 2 s + final 2 s ≈ 15–19 s — under the 30 s minimum. The proposal says "Plays scroll in slowly" but Pre-Colors has no play-by-play data, only scoring events. Either reduce playInterval to ~0.25 s and add non-scoring play lines, or accept a ~20 s game (contradicts the 30–60 s spec). Need a play-count calculation.

3. **No Skip interruption logic.** "Clicking Skip jumps to final state instantly" but the scheduled `setTimeout` chain has no clearTimeout/cancel mechanism. A click during Q1 would queue the final state while Q1 lines keep appearing. Fix: track pending timers and abort on Skip.

4. **No CONTINUE handler specified.** Step 5 says "wait for CONTINUE click" but no DOM element, selector, or click handler is defined. Fix: add the CONTINUE button markup and handler.

5. **`window.matchMedia` can throw.** `window.matchMedia('(prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)').matches` throws if matchMedia is undefined. Fix: guard with `typeof window.matchMedia !== 'undefined' && ...matches`.

6. **Quarter dead air.** 10 s per quarter but only ~2–3 scoring events per quarter at 0.8 s = ~2 s content. ~8 s of dead air per quarter block. The timeline needs filler or shorter quarters.

7. **Clutch moment time unit undefined.** Events use `t` (a fraction, not minutes). "within 7 after the 10-minute mark" can't be computed from `t` without a conversion. Fix: define t's unit or compute clutch from q+t.

8. **Halftime/top-stats unimplemented.** "brief stats (top rusher/passer)" has no extraction code from `g.detail`.
