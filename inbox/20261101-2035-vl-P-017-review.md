| 4-3 slot | 3-4 slot | Rating uses |
|----------|----------|-------------|

| DT2 | DT2 | DT slotWins |
| — | ILB1 | **MIKE** slotWins (ILB ≡ MIKE) |

| — | OLB1 | **WILL** slotWins (OLB ≡ WILL) |

**OLB ≡ WILL** in wins maths: OLB players use WILL's slotWins/effOvr when assigned to OLB slots.
**ILB ≡ MIKE** in wins maths: ILB players use MIKE's slotWins/effOvr when assigned to ILB slots.
