The layout covers most requested sections, but the mock-up is not safe to use as an implementation reference without correcting several conflicts with current product rules and labeling/removing unsupported data.

No source, calculation, or synthetic-data label accompanies them. Under the data-integrity rule, remove these claims or label and authorize any illustrative values before they are shown.

- **Weakest point:** this is a design reference whose exact copy includes both behaviorally incorrect flex advice and unsourced quantitative claims; those errors could be mistaken for approved product rules or verified model output during implementation.
