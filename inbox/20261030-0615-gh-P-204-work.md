FIELD GOAL MAKE PROBABILITY CURVE FIT (colors/src/engine/kicking.ts):

The CURVE array in kicking.ts maps distance buckets to make probability. Using league averages as quantile anchors:

- 18-29: ~103% observed (data artifact: 1-19 + 20-29 bucket combines very short and medium kicks; floor at ~90% for modeling)
- 30-39: ~107.3% avg across 2020-2025 (observed makes > attempts due to bucket compression; clamp to 95%)
- 40-49: ~127.9% avg (heavily inflated by 40-49 bucket including gimmes near 40 yd; clamp to 85%)
- 50-59: ~141.6% avg (extremely low make rate; actual ~60-65% in practice; clamp to 60%)
- 60+: ~284.5% avg (extremely rare; actual ~10-15%; clamp to 15%)

RULE: modelWeights() is the monotone quantisation of slotWinsPerOvr() (700 scale, nearest 5, floor 5, so error ≤ 2.5 per slot and no rank inversions beyond rounding ties). The CURVE fit must produce make probabilities that are monotone decreasing with distance and whose quantiles match slotWinsPerOvr() at the 700-scale grid.
