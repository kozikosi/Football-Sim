### 1. uk's comparison is thorough but misses `flexcheck.mjs` output
The P-193 brief explicitly asks: "one-way rules that look like slips (`flexcheck.mjs` prints them)". uk's work file does not mention `flexcheck.mjs` at all. The tool output shows 24 ONE-WAY rules:
```
QB->FS TE->C TE->OG TE->OT TE->DT OT->FB OT->RB OG->FB OG->RB
C->FB C->RB DT->FB DT->RB EDGE->FB EDGE->RB ILB->FB OLB->FB
SS->RB SS->FB FS->RB CB->ILB CB->OLB CB->TE CB->RB
```
Some are intentional (QB→FS only, no reverse). Others look like slips:
- **OT→FB and OT→RB** exist but FB→OT and RB→OT don't. Is a FB or RB flexing to OT ever useful? Probably not, so one-way is fine.
- **DT→FB and DT→RB** exist but FB→DT and RB→DT don't. A FB flexing to DT is unrealistic. OK.
- **EDGE→FB and EDGE→RB** exist but reverse doesn't. OK.
- **CB→ILB, CB→OLB, CB→TE, CB→RB** exist but reverse doesn't. A TE flexing to CB is unrealistic. OK.
- **SS→RB and SS→FB** exist but reverse doesn't. OK.
- **FS→RB** exists but RB→FS doesn't. OK.
The one-way rules are mostly defensible (flexing from a bigger position to a smaller one is allowed; reverse is not). But the brief asks for them to be listed. uk omitted this.

### 2. uk's DIME claim is partially wrong
uk says: "REV 4 adds DIME to ILB and OLB (REV 2 only has DIME as destination for WR, TE, SS, FS)". But the Owner's guide actually lists DIME for ILB and OLB:
- "MIKE / ILB: ... -6 DE, TE, S, NICKEL, DIME"
- "WILL / SAM / OLB: ... -6 DT, TE, S, FB, DIME"
So DIME is in the guide for ILB and OLB. uk's claim that REV 4 "adds" it is incorrect. The guide already includes it.

### 3. uk's QB "S" interpretation is ambiguous
uk's table lists QB→SS: -6 and QB→FS: -6. But the Owner's guide says "QB: -6 WR, S" — a single "S", not "SS" and "FS" separately. uk assumed both SS and FS. The "S" could mean either SS or FS (or both). This ambiguity should be flagged. REV 4 correctly resolves it: QB→FS only at -10.

### 4. Gaming of QB-to-S at -6 (REV 2)
The P-193 brief asks: "any way QB-to-S at -6 can be gamed". In REV 2, QB could flex to "S" (SS or FS) at -6, which is cheaper than the REV 4 FS-only at -10. A user could:
- Flex QB to SS at -6 (instead of FS at -10) to get a better deal.
- Since "S" is ambiguous, a user could argue that SS qualifies, getting a 4-point advantage over REV 4.
- Even worse: if "S" means either SS or FS, the user picks the cheaper one.
REV 4 fixes this by removing QB→SS and QB→WR, and increasing QB→FS to -10. This is the correct fix and should be noted.
