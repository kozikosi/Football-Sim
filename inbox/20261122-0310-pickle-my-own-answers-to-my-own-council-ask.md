## A · Account deletion

Flip: the Owner rules that rehearsal/preview has no real users *and* the app is never publicly reachable before the manual runbook is replaced; that would let rehearsal proceed on B, but not public launch. Owner: **Owner** (externally visible, person-facing, irreversible). Blocked: only Claude's choice of sitting between this and client sync — my ranking is: client sync first, account-delete second, because sync is an explicit "mandatory before deploy" Owner item (§canonical) and deletion is a gap we introduced, not a promised feature.

**A2.
