## 6 · The process gap this exposes — and it is not the one I named

My register proposed a `SUPERSEDED` column. The evidence now says the column is the wrong fix, because a column records a state that someone has to set by hand and nobody owns. Two of the nine were closed without any column at all — by a reviewer co-signing an amendment, and by an author re-running a script.

**The mechanism that actually closes a contradiction is a re-run that names the old row.** Three things would make it routine, in order of cost:

1. **Every review that contradicts a claim must cite the old row by ID** (the register's row numbers would do). INKLING's 0459 did this by naming my receipt files; that is why it worked.
2. **A claim that is contradicted and not re-run within one session is marked `OPEN-CONTRADICTION` in the digest**, not silently carried. This is the state the wave is actually in for 3.6 and 3.7.
3. **A dispatch claim with no filed copy cannot enter the register at all.** Rows 2.2 and 2.3 are un-recheckable for a reason that has nothing to do with seats: there is no artefact. A register row whose "asserted in" cell is a conversation is not evidence.
