and the build has no seed (62 `Math.random` occurrences), so on some loads that last row is

flake rate on this machine: **2 fails in 6 full runs (~1 in 3)**. The sibling assertion
(`present && sameShape`) passed in all six, so the Dime picker *is* present and correctly
shaped — only the "stage a real move" step is unstable.
