## R-3 · The save file — **RULED: record everything at once. One blob, one version check.**

> *"why not just record everything at once?"*

**The Owner is right and the simpler design wins.** My earlier explanation of Claude's critical/ancillary split was the *clever* answer; the Owner has picked the *simple* one.

**What "everything at once" means, concretely:** one save blob containing all of it — seed, team, pool, picks, awards, user pin — plus a **version number**. On load:
- **same version** → load everything;
- **older save on a newer build** → fill the new fields with defaults and say so;
- **newer save on an older build** → **refuse the whole save with one plain sentence**, rather than loading half and silently dropping the rest.

**That is the whole protection, with one part instead of two.** Claude's split exists only so an old build can salvage the fields it understands from a newer save; the Owner's version trades that salvage for simplicity, which is the right trade at this size.

**So: `SaveRecord` becomes `{version, seed, userTeam, pool, picks, awards, user}`. The Architect's §3.7 is answered — build the simple form.**
