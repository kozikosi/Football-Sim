## DEFECTS (shown — rule text is materially wrong)
1. **"No coin toss" — false.** NFL overtime begins with a coin toss (winner receives). gh's Regular Season bullet 1 says "no coin toss".
2. **"10-minute periods" garbled.** Regular season OT is ONE 10-minute period (shortened from 15 in 2017), not "periods" or tied to "the first quarter". gh writes "Game ends after the first quarter's overtime: 10-minute periods" — incoherent.
3. **Playoff rule wrong.** gh says playoffs are "Sudden death from the start: first team to score wins". Since the 2022 rule change, playoff OT guarantees BOTH teams at least one possession; repeat periods are played until a winner (no ties). gh also says "both teams receive an initial possession (except when a touchdown ends game immediately)" in the same paragraph — internally contradictory with its own "sudden death from the start".
4. **First-possession TD handling muddled.** gh: "If first team scores a touchdown (any result: touchdown, 2-pt conv, FG): game ends immediately." An FG is not a touchdown; after a walk-off TD the game ends (no XP/2-pt attempt). gh conflates these.
5. **"Games ~1,032 per season" — false.** An NFL season has 272 games (32 teams × 17 games / 2). gh's "Games" column (1,032–1,100) has no basis; the whole REAL OVERTIME OUTCOMES table is therefore unverifiable and likely fabricated.
6. **Sources not citable.** "2026 NFL Official Rulebook, Sections 1, 5, 9 (verified from NFL.com rules page)" — no URL, no page; and the rule content is wrong, so the claimed verification is unsupported. All frequency numbers (OT reach 7.5-8.2%, first-possession TD 46.8-49.5%, ties 16.5-18.3%) lack any citable source. Per AGENTS.md §7: source per number or label UNKNOWN.
