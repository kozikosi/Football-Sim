## Findings

**1. DEFECT (new) — the fallback `#d4af37` can never render; the marker would be `#ffc43c`.**
`var(--gold,#d4af37)` was written "until the theme tokens (P-003) are built", but `--gold` is **already defined** on Pre-Colors: line 217 `:root{ --glow:rgba(79,140,255,.28); --gold:#ffc43c; }`. Measured on the patched copy: `getComputedStyle(:root).getPropertyValue('--gold')` → `#ffc43c`, and the cue renders `rgb(255,196,60)`.
`#ffc43c` is a yellow-gold, and the Owner has ruled against exactly that drift: *"no orange anywhere (gold must not drift orange)"* (V23-OWNER-REQUESTS line 6) and P-058 *"more shimmering gold, less snot yellow"*. A dead fallback hides the mismatch instead of showing it.
Fix: write `var(--gold)` with **no** fallback, so the marker inherits whatever the Colors v1 palette sets, and let the palette item (Q-V23-2 / P-058) own the value. Do not hardcode `#d4af37` here either: it is the dark-theme prototype's gold, and light mode needs a deeper gold by the same ruling.

**2. DEFECT (new) — `★` is the star system the Owner is removing.**
`★` is the priority-star glyph (`PRIO_STAR`, line 1572, and its help text at 1708/4994), and **P-043 "Remove Star system from draft board" removes it**. Reusing it as a "better player" mark re-introduces in one panel the symbol the Owner wants gone in another, and reads as the retired star rating rather than "better". The brief only says *"small gold marker"*.
Fix: use a non-star glyph. I tested `◆`: 11.5 px wide, inline, no layout effect. Any non-star mark works; the point is that it is not `★`/`☆`.

**3. DEFECT (new) — the patch does not make the page neutral, and the review says it does.**
Beam notes `unitComparisonHTML` "should probably" change but leaves it out of the snippet. Measured on the Home → Team Comparison tab, patched: **14 cells still render `rgb(62,207,142)`** — 0 inside the lineup table, **14 inside the Unit Comparison table below it**. So on the same screen the lineup is neutral and the units below are still green.
The same green cue also sits at line 3754 (`const grn = 'font-weight:700;color:var(--good);'`, the game-preview unit-grade table). There are 22 `var(--good)` sites in the file; only 3358, 3360 and 3380 are comparison tables. Whoever builds P-008 should decide once whether "the better side" is gold everywhere (my recommendation, with P-046/P-064 already removing green) or green outside lineups.

**4. CLARIFICATION — one change covers three screens.**
`renderLineupTableHTML` has exactly three call sites: line 3419 (Home → Team Comparison), 3804 (game preview "Lineups"), 6396 (results → Team Comparison). Beam does not say this; it is the strongest part of the proposal and worth the Architect knowing it is a single-function edit, not three.

**5. CALIBRATION QUESTION — the cue ranks *effective* rating, not raw OVR.**
`lineupCell` returns `val: eff` (`raw[3]-flexPenaltyFor(...)`, line 3349), i.e. the post-flex number the table prints. The brief says "higher OVR". Effective is the honest cue (it matches the printed number and the note under the table), so I keep it — but ties produce **no** marker on either side, and a flexed player may lose the mark to a worse raw OVR. Confirm that is intended.
