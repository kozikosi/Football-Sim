--- SITEWIDE STYLE LAW (V23) ---

1. NO WHITE TEXT. Body text is warm white (#f5f0f0 on dark, #2a1a20 on light). Numbers and accents are gold (#d4af37 dark, #b8941f light). Never pure white (#ffffff) for body text. White (#f5f0f0) is only for headings and emphasis.

2. INTEGERS ONLY. All displayed numbers are integers (no decimals). OVR, wins, points, percentages — all rounded to whole numbers. Exception: win probability can show one decimal (e.g., 62.3%).

3. CINZEL DECORATIVE for display type. Page titles, section headers, team names, and champion banners use Cinzel Decorative (or similar serif display font) loaded via @font-face (no CDN). Body text uses system-ui.

4. TITLES AND SUBTITLES FADE IN AND OUT, SLOWLY. Page titles and section headers fade in (opacity 0→1, 800ms ease) on view enter, fade out (opacity 1→0, 400ms ease) on view leave. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (instant).

5. ONE-OR-TWO-WORD BUTTONS FULLY CAPITALISED. Button labels with 1-2 words are ALL CAPS (e.g., "START DRAFT", "SIMULATE GAME", "UNDO"). Multi-word buttons (3+) use Title Case.

6. FOIL SHIMMER FOR 90+ AND AWARDS. Players with 90+ OVR and award winners (MVP, DPOY, etc.) get a foil shimmer effect (gold/rose gradient, 2.5s ease-in-out infinite). Lower tiers: solid colour, no shimmer.

7. HOVER NAMES FOR POSITION CODES. Position codes (QB, RB, WR, etc.) show the full position name on hover (title attribute or tooltip). E.g., "WR" → "Wide Receiver", "CB" → "Cornerback".

8. NO "SEE GUIDE FOR DETAILS" AND NO "BACK TO SIMULATOR" ANYWHERE. These phrases are banned. Use "Read the guide" or "Back" instead. The guide is always accessible via the tab bar.

9. NO BLUE PANELS. All panels use the rose/gold/white palette (open/airy glass). No blue (#4f8cff, #007bff, #6aa6ff) in panels, borders, or backgrounds.

10. NO ORANGE. Gold must not drift orange. Use #b8941f (light) / #d4af37 (dark) for gold. Champion banner gold: #8a6a10 → #b8941f (clean gold).

11. NO EMOJIS. No emoji characters anywhere in the product.

12. OPEN/AIRY DESIGN. Glass panels (rgba(255,255,255,0.04) + blur), hairline borders (rgba(255,255,255,0.08)), generous padding (20-24px), no heavy shadows by default.

13. VALUE HERE IS BACKGROUND ONLY. Never displayed in any visual element (no column, no label, no sort option). Used only for Priority calculation.

14. TEAM COLOURS ON TEAM ELEMENTS ONLY. Global rose/gold/white is the default. When a team is selected, their colours override on team-specific elements (tags, score boxes, avatars). Player names use warm white, not team colour.

15. NO PERMANENT GREEN. Green (#22c55e) must not appear anywhere. Use gold for positive indicators, red for negative.

16. REDUCED MOTION RESPECTED. All animations disabled under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce. Audio is separate but toggle persists.

17. MOBILE FLOOR: 390px. No horizontal scroll. Primary actions above fold. Dropdowns stay side-by-side (shrink, don't stack).

18. SHIMMER TEXT FOR EMPHASIS. Key phrases (page titles, champion banners, award winners, "Can you go 20-0?") use shimmering gradient text. This is the canonical example of emphasizing a word in the colour palette.
