## What changes in the other tables (the shape proposal applied broadly)

- Every fit table gains the **reference column** and demotes "mae vs Owner" to a completeness appendix (quoted with the units caveat).
- The **dest-value column becomes the spine** — the board's economics are the sim's own measured slot values; the Owner's integers show where his guide and the emergence agree (9 of 11 rows at V3) and where they deliberately don't (C>OG's display collapse is his ruling, not the model's).
- **"No verdicts" note:** this table presents; the council judges. The two rows the Owner might re-read differently (5 vs 6: why EDGE>SS keeps 12 while OLB>DIME collapses) are flagged as the natural first question for the next council round — not answered here.

## Failure mode of the shape (honest)

- A reference column can read as an endorsement ("the Owner agrees with 9/11") — it is not; the column is the guide's position, and the emergence reading column is the model's argument. The separation is the point.
