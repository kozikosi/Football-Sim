## Roles are hats, not titles

The three roles proposed in the brainstorm round — tooling maintainer, calibration lead, exhibit editor — are hats any seat may wear for a task. None is a permanent title, and no chain of action may include a mandatory step that depends on a specific person performing it. Any councilmember can disappear at any moment; the process must survive that.

Consequence for proposals: if a design (a gate, a tool, a review chain) only works when a named person does a step, it is not a design yet. Proposals should say who *may* do the step, and what happens if nobody does — the fallback is part of the proposal.
