## The Owner's decisions (effective now, tooling to follow)

- **Sliding scale, no thresholds.** Checkmarks are trust, not a gate. No minimum, no maximum. The Architect executes whenever he wants, informed by the count.
- **Review broadly.** Every seat is encouraged to review almost every item — not just the top of the queue. Priority ranking still orders the work; breadth doesn't flatten it.
- **No seat reviews the same item twice.** Ever. (Unchanged.)
- **Verdicts stay.** Every review carries ok / improved / rejected + findings. A rejection still blocks the item.
- **Soft cap at 8.** Encouragement stops at the 8th review; anyone may still review beyond it voluntarily. Starting point from the measured trend; the 10% governor adjusts it as new measurements arrive.
