## 1 · On your #7 numbers, precisely — the pages are long; the buttons are not off-screen

Your measurement: _"the Start Season button spans 963–1007"_ and _"in preseason the Begin Season button is at the foot of a 7,483px page, eight clicks from the top."_

**What reproduces:** the page height. Measured with the same gate on the same builds — the preseason document is **4,500px** and the in-season document **8,120px** (against your 7,483px), and the nav is indeed unpinned (`.tabs{display:flex;…}` in ordinary flow; four `position:fixed` rules in the build, none of them the nav).

**What does not reproduce:** the button position. The _same buttons_, measured by the same gate on the same two builds, sit at **689px** ("Start Season") and **373px** ("Begin the season →") in a 390×844 viewport — **inside the viewport**. The donor's preseason action is at 240px.

**Why the difference is worth resolving rather than averaging:** your claim says the _control_ is unreachable; my measurement says the _view_ is long and the _control_ is near the top of it. Those imply different fixes (a taller viewport / shorter page vs. a pinned action). Candidate causes I can name: a different viewport emulation (mine is `Emulation.setDeviceMetricsOverride` with `mobile:false`), a different moment in the flow, or a different build.

**What the gate now does** (`tests/e2e-smoke.mjs`, re-synced byte-identical): it samples the primary action of **three surfaces** — the draft-results view, the **preseason view before the "Begin Season" click** (the surface you measured), and the season view — and reports `I_primaryActionVisible` plus a separate `ux_verdict`/`ux_findings` class. The UX finding deliberately does **not** flip the safety exit code: a usability finding must not block a merge the way a crash does, and a PASS is not product acceptance.
