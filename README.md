# 76 Pancakes

Can you go 20-0?

76 Pancakes is a football draft and season sim. You draft a team, you play a season, you try to go 20-0. Seventeen regular-season wins and three playoff wins. That's the whole game.

## How it works

Draft a 27-man roster from real NFL players. Real names, real teams. The ratings aren't EA's — they're ours. Then sim the season.

The engine is top-down. Team ratings produce the outcome, then the stats get distributed. No button-mashing. No stick skills. You win by drafting well, not by reacting faster.

The draft is about value, not raw overall. A player is worth what he wins you at his spot. A corner who shuts down a WR1 is worth more than a guard with a bigger number. That's the philosophy: stop their WR1, stop their WR2, stop the slot, stop the tight end from moving the chains, pressure the quarterback, stop the run. In that order.
