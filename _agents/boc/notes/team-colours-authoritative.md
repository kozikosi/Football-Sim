## Display rules (from MOC's colour-identity system)
- edge → primary, or secondary when primary < 1.5 contrast on panel
- stripe → primary → tertiary with secondary as blend stop, never as lone mark
- text → app's --text, team colour carried by chip's tint and border
- gold stays gold
- Keep white in brand_colors; never require it as visible edge colour
