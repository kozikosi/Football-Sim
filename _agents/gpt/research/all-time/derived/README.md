# Derived all-time candidate work

This directory holds reviewable derived candidate records. The source files under raw/ remain the owner’s original notes and are not rewritten.

## Sport-separated files

- nfl/official-all-time-gap-candidates.csv — 17 NFL players selected for the official NFL 100 All-Time Team but not found in the eight supplied NFL source files after checking names and common aliases.
- nba/official-all-time-gap-candidates.csv — Sam Jones, the NBA 75th Anniversary Team player not found in the supplied NBA notes. Other NBA 75 names were present under full names or familiar shorthand (for example, “Mailman” and “Moses”).

The league honors lists are a transparent omission screen, not a final all-time ranking or a claim that these are the only omissions. Candidates remain proposed until the owner reviews their eligibility and historical position mapping.

## Status, seasons, and ratings

- These two addition files contain retired players. Current or recent player mentions remain in the sport-specific status-triage/nfl/ and status-triage/nba/ queues. A recent peak-season marker is not proof of an active roster spot.
- A person’s career status is separate from a player-season candidate. Do not infer a player's current status from the season used for a peak.
- season, team, and engine_ovr are intentionally blank where the cited source does not provide that exact record. Never fill those values by guess.
- No ratings are assigned in these files. The current NFL engine stores integer OVR in the fourth PLAYERS tuple field; NBA 2K values must remain source-specific until the owner approves a conversion to the engine scale.
