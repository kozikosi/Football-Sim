- `colors/`: TypeScript source, no runtime dependencies. `src/engine/` is pure (no DOM, no Math.random, seeded `Rng`), `src/ui/` is the page, `build/build.mjs` strips types (TypeScript's own `transpileModule`) and emits **one self-contained `Football-Sim-Colors-v1.html`** (the delivered artifact stays one file; AGENTS.md §3 reading confirmed by the Owner's earlier answer).
- Tests: `node --test --experimental-strip-types "test/*.test.ts"` (VERIFIED working, 10 pass). `npm install` is unreachable in this sandbox (registry ENOTFOUND, VERIFIED), so no esbuild/Vitest; the global TypeScript is enough.
- Data: `data/madden27-ratings.csv` (1,941) + `data/free-agents-old-ratings.csv` (12) compiled into the build; players are identified by `name|pos|team`, never name alone (3 duplicate names).
- Colour: the 32-team table (3 hexes each, Owner's 22 Oct table; read from `ZPB_TEAM_COLORS` in w33 and checked against the Owner's table before use). Per-team UI palette: neutral, tinted, brightened when dark; the algorithm is a port of w33's `zpbPackFor` so the homepage dropdown colours match, tested for contrast. Fallback theme: soft rose near-white, gold accents. Light/dark toggle fixed in the footer on every page.

## Step 6 notes (Architect, 30 Oct 2026): drive generator
- `colors/src/engine/drives.ts` (`simulateGame(mh, ma, rng, {playoff, homeField?, otLimit?})`) + `test/drives.test.ts` (9 tests; 111 pass in the suite). Realised totals as Pre-Colors (rush/pass lognormal, Poisson giveaways and sacks, points from the realised totals), then the score is split into TDs/conversions/FGs/defensive TDs/safeties and laid onto the drives; yards, plays, sacks and the 3,600 s are shared out by largest remainder so identities are exact: events sum = score; regulation drive yards/sacks/giveaways = realised totals; clock = 3,600 (+ overtime); takeaways = opponent giveaways; drives alternate in time order.
- **Overtime (VERIFIED by search 30 Oct, onpattison.com report of the Eagles proposal):** since 2025 BOTH teams get a possession in regular-season overtime too, 10-minute period. This replaces the older assumption "first TD ends it" (master_plan 4). Implemented: both possess (unless a defensive score ends it), then sudden death; a second-team TD that puts them ahead is a walk-off (6 pts, no conversion); playoffs: 15-minute periods until a winner; regular season: tie when 10 minutes run out (tie rule INFERRED, the source is silent). `otLimit` exists only so tests can force period roll-over.
- **INFERRED, pending council P-204/P-205/P-206:** ~11.4 drives a team (both teams get the same number), 26 s a play, share of drives by ending, 12% of giveaways returned for a TD, 15% of field goals missed, 55% chance a half ends on a clock-out drive, overtime drive odds. Measured on 2,000 level games: points, yards, drives, giveaways, sacks, home edge, overtime rate and TDs per team inside the bands in the test (NFL-like); bands, not a calibration.
- **Not in this step:** Pre-Colors' late-game "script" (trailing team throws, leader runs) and mixed plans (`choosePlans/samplePlans`); DL5 short-yardage snaps (needs player-level plays); kicker accuracy by distance (P-204); per-player attribution (step 9). Penalties: none (Owner).
- **Mutation check:** clock total, second overtime possession, playoff period roll-over, TD value, giveaway rate each made a test fail; the roll-over one survived at first and led to the `otLimit` hook plus a dedicated test.
