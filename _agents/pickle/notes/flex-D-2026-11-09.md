## Findings

### D-PICKLE-01 (VERIFIED) — the WILL/C weight bump moves D by exactly 0 under the engine's slope
`slotWins()` (`colors/src/sports/nfl/engine/value.ts:25`) builds ratings from `fieldSlots(...).map(f => ({row, ovr}))`;
`teamRatings` weights by `RATING_SPECS`, never by `SLOTS[].weight`. So `slotWins20` — the slope in `thetaprice.mjs`
— is independent of the slot weights.

```
$ node --experimental-strip-types --no-warnings /tmp/dtest.mjs   # mutates WILL/C weight 150→200, re-measures
weights before WILL 150 C 150
WILL 0.394449 0.394449 … SS 0.788303 0.788303 … C 0.375202 0.375202
max |delta slope after WILL/C bump = 0
```
Confirmed inside the rebuild: `Weight bumps move 0 rule(s) in D (max |delta D| 0)` (wins slope).

**Consequence for P-311 step 2** ("check that D was recomputed AFTER the WILL/C bumps"): recomputation is a
**no-op**. A reviewer who expects different D values after the bump will either report a false failure or
silently accept a table built on pre-bump D. The bump still matters for the draft and lineup (weight is used
there); it does not touch the flex arithmetic.

### D-PICKLE-02 (VERIFIED) — the Owner's "~6.7 after the bump" is true only under a *weight-ratio* slope
Under the weight reading (`slope = SLOTS[].weight`), the bump does move 8 rules, and it produces exactly the
Owner's numbers: `OLB>ILB 10.00 → 6.67`, `OLB>SS 10.00 → 6.67` → both round to 6. Under the engine's measured
slope they are `10.05` and `9.99` → 12, bump or no bump.

So the Owner's −6 for OLB>ILB / OLB>SS is **correct as an override, not as a rounding result** under the
engine's own D. The two readings agree on every headline rule to within 0.4 (TE>EDGE 12.71 vs 12.31,
RB>WR 12.32 vs 12.31, TE>OT 10.03 vs 10.00, DT>EDGE 4.61 vs 4.62), because the slot weights were fitted to
the measured curve — which is why the discrepancy went unnoticed.

### D-PICKLE-03 (VERIFIED) — three final rules depend on which slope definition is "the" D
| rule | wins slope | weight slope | Owner named? |
|---|---|---|---|
| `TE>FS` | 6 | 2 | no |
| `TE>SS` | 6 | 2 | no |
| `OG>OT` | 2 | 6 | no |

Every other disagreement (OLB>ILB, OLB>SS, RB>FS, RB>SS, TE>ILB) is already overridden by the Owner, so it
does not reach the table. **Recommendation:** use the wins slope (it is the engine's own measurement and the
one behind the four published numbers) and let the Owner name these three if he cares; do not let the two
definitions pick silently.

### D-PICKLE-04 (VERIFIED) — free heavy→light rules are the risk surface, not the removals
The 30 free rules include moves a viewer may not expect: `EDGE>OT 0`, `EDGE>OG 0`, `EDGE>RB 0`,
`EDGE>ILB 0`, `DT>C 0`, `CB>SS 0`. These are the formula saying "the destination is the heavier spot", and the
Owner accepted that reading ("mostly moves from a heavier spot to a lighter spot where D=0"). They are the
rules a draft artifact would use, so they are the first thing P-312 should scan for — not the 7 removals.
