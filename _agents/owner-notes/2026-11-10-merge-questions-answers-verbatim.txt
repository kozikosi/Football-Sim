- Q1 "together": the merged formula keeps BOTH blend parameters — Z.ai's λ (0.4, the weight on the sum of the non-worst gaps) AND Claude's exponent k (the soft-max dial from "worst binds" to "add them up"). Do NOT collapse them into one dial. The designers determine the combined form (e.g., a k-norm over the worst gap and the λ-weighted sum of the rest, or another two-parameter shape). No hand-authored NEED tables for new positions (Z.ai's method). No hand-picked mix coefficients (Claude's method). Pricing SS2 and the DL5 blitzer after someone specifies them is not enough.

## Fourth note (2026-11-10, same thread)

Snap share (τ): "theres no way for the formula to intuit snap share and that type of number isn't consistent throughout all sports. i don't know"

## MUSE's reading (not the Owner's words — for the record)

- Snap share (τ) — the authored per-role number in Z.ai's derived value function v(r) = τ·Σ NEED·w·scarcity² — cannot be INTUITED by the formula (it is an empirical fact about how often a role is on the field, not derivable from what the job demands) AND is not consistent across sports (football has snaps; basketball/soccer have minutes with different distributions; baseball has plate appearances/innings — there is no single "snap share" with a consistent meaning). So a value layer that depends on τ is football-shaped and does not port. This is a point FOR the measured value layer (Claude's — measure the slot value from the sim, which captures playing time implicitly, no τ needed) and AGAINST the derived value layer (Z.ai's — needs τ authored per role). The Owner's "i don't know" is uncertainty, not a ruling — he is flagging the problem, not solving it. The merge prompt's Q2 is updated to reflect this: the derived v's τ is a portability problem, and the direction is measured v (with socialism handling the FB snap-weighting issue). The valuing-a-new-role problem (Q8) remains open, but τ-based derivation is out as a method.
