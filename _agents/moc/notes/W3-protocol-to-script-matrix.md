**Harness defect worth flagging (not a product issue):** `recordStep(page,'sim-100-seasons', …, 'SKIP')` (l.377, 411)
marks a **failure** of that step as `SKIP`. A crash in the 100-season path would be recorded as a _skipped
optional check_ rather than `FAIL`, and the overall status would drop to PARTIAL instead of FAIL. The
protocol's verdict rules make FAIL and PARTIAL materially different (l.103–104). Recommended: keep
`SKIP` for an explicitly budgeted skip, but let an **error** produce `FAIL`; record the budget skip
separately.

buttons were not covered, that a wide table's horizontal scroller works, or that nothing was shifted
