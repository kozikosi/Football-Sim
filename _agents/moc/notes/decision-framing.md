## What Claude must not do

1. **Do not invent** player ratings, team assignments, or statistical outputs.
2. **Do not hand-edit evidence files.** The receipts are append-only.
3. **Do not break `modelWeights() = f(slotWinsPerOvr())`.** The Guide's value table is the **model's output**, not a table to type into.
4. **Do not change a protected constant** — `DEFAULT_WEIGHTS`, `FLEX`, `NATURAL_SLOT_MAP`, the `MC` coefficients, `PLAN_K`, `RATING_SPECS` — without the Owner's approval. **The proposed ≈ −5 current-player adjustment is not approved.**
5. **No build steps, no CDN dependencies, no paid APIs.** The build stays a single file.
6. **Do not call a change verified without a seeded comparison.** The build has 58 unseeded `Math.random()` sites, so two raw runs are not a comparison: install a seeded `Math.random` via `Page.addScriptToEvaluateOnNewDocument` before the page's scripts run, then drive the same clicks in both builds.
7. **Do not colour a team without a source.**
8. **Do not run `merge-html-applier.js`** — it is incomplete and its deletions are destructive.

## The five questions for the Owner, consolidated

1. **The weight deltas.** *Choice:* apply the Owner's five numbers (K 0.45, WR2 0.63, RB1 0.58, CB2 0.95, DE1 1.38) through the model's coefficients, plus TE1 within 0.5 of WR1, TE2 within 0.5 of WR3, and a run share for QB and the receivers — or leave the weights as they are. *Why:* it is the change that moves every number in the product, and the per-spot table is what the Owner reads as "the game's balance". *Default:* apply them through the model, and re-validate the draft board, the 100-season report and the real-2025 check (r ≈ 0.66, ρ ≈ 0.69, RMSE ≈ 2.6) before it ships. *Cost of being wrong:* high — the balance is wrong and every downstream number inherits it. *Now?* **Yes** — it blocks the most, and it is the only one that needs the Owner's approval before work continues.
2. **The team colours' source.** *Choice:* colour only the user's own team, or supply a citable brand source for the 32 real teams. *Why:* the whole aesthetics section is mechanism-only without a source, and unsourced colours are presented to users as fact. *Default:* colour only the user's own team, now, and add the 32 when a source is citable. *Cost:* medium — an aesthetic delay, not a correctness problem. *Now?* **Yes** for the source answer; the mechanism can be built meanwhile.
3. **The stars.** *Choice:* remove the star system, and accept that the board's ranking changes; or keep it. *Why:* the Owner called it "not useful and confusing", but the stars multiply Priority by ×1.5/×1.0/×0.5, so removing them re-orders the board. *Default:* remove, and report the measured ranking change with the change. *Cost:* medium — the Owner's draft board looks different. *Now?* **Yes** for the change, with the measurement attached.
4. **The NBA pool.** *Choice:* the Owner's list as written (539 records, his spellings) with positions and teams only from citable sources — or a smaller, fully-sourced pool. *Why:* 63 players are still unmatched against current rosters, and the pool cannot be completed without a source. *Default:* the Owner's list as written; the unmatched players stay flagged rather than guessed. *Cost:* high if guessed; low if flagged. *Now?* **Yes** — one consolidated question, and the NBA build waits on it.
5. **The badges.** *Choice:* the per-team chips, or the three penalty sections ("Can't Run Penalty", "Weak D Penalty", "Can't Pass Penalty"). *Why:* the Owner's example points at the penalties, but the instruction ("move the badges… under 'Back to my weights'") points at the chips, and they are different elements in different places. *Default:* the per-team chips, in a strip under the two buttons, with an empty state. *Cost:* low — a layout. *Now?* **No** — one line when convenient; everything else can proceed.

**Everything else is defaulted above and needs no answer.** Items 1, 3, 4, 7 and 10 are Claude's to implement on the stated default.
