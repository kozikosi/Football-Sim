## What this changes in the plan

1. **Scope is now 21, not 8.** The engine and the data pipeline are the same work; the *content* is 21
   simulations in the order above. NFL 2027 and NFL all-time stay first, as the Owner's priority 1 requires.
2. **The soccer-eligibility question is answered.** The leagues are named: EPL, Champions League, La Liga,
   Bundesliga, Serie A, Ligue 1 — with MLS in the low-priority set. No ranking source is needed to *choose*
   them; a source is only needed to **order and seed** them, and the Owner's order above now does that.
3. **WNBA 2027 is #5**, above the European football leagues — worth flagging because it is the first
   women's sport and the first that is not American-football-shaped, so the draft and rotation design will
   be asked to stretch.
4. **The low-priority items are single-season only**, and the expectation is that they come cheaply
   *because the engine already exists* for their sport. That is an argument for building the engine
   generally — a sport profile with its own maths — which is also the Owner's stated architecture direction.
5. **Baseball and ice hockey each get a current and an all-time version** in the top 21, so the all-time
   data model must cover two more sports beyond football and basketball.
