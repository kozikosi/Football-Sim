## Findings (the w88 lesson — reported, not hidden)

1. **The snapshot's `app/feed.ts` `clock()` does not handle overtime starts.** A drive starting at 3600s+ maps to `Q4` with a NEGATIVE mm:ss remainder (`Math.min(4, ceil(secs/900))` + `900*q - secs`). The feed therefore renders period≥5 drives with an `OT` stamp and the quarter marks say `OT` — presentation working AROUND a frozen-snapshot quirk, never editing it. **Filed for the Architect:** one line in a future re-pin (`q = min(4, …)` → an OT branch), or the feed adopts the fixed mapper verbatim.
2. **The saves panel shows the WHOLE league log count** (the envelope law: the record carries every pick, CPU and user). At the user's first turn that reads "20 picks" — correct per the payload contract, potentially surprising next to the footer's "0 of 32 starters" (which counts the user's fills only). The panel label now reads the raw log count deliberately; a friendlier split (yours + theirs) is an `IDEA:` below, not a change to the payload.
3. **`goneOdds` cost:** the App recomputes 30 sims on every user-turn refresh. Measured fine at the board's scale (the battery runs 60+ refreshes in seconds), but the season screens should reuse one odds computation per pick rather than per render. Noted for the season slice.
4. **Partial-roster ratings:** `feedVM.teamRatingsOf` rates whatever roster it is given; on an unfinished draft that would rate incomplete lineups. The PLAY WEEK 1 gate (`status === 'done'`) makes the path unreachable from the UI, and the battery asserts the gate. Kept as a guard, documented here.
5. **The flex-chip count is state-dependent** (the P-314 finding): the battery keeps asserting the OBSERVATION (chips read the `−N flex` form while a picker is open), never a fixed count — this run saw 44 with one seed.

## Innovations (beyond the list — the autonomy grant, say-what-and-why)

1. **The method tooltip carries the engine's actual numbers** — not a static explainer. `picksBetween` and `sims` ride down from the very `goneOdds` object the board renders, and the battery proves the tooltip equals a node-side recompute. The shell's carried idea was "a tooltip explaining its method"; the built version can't drift from the engine because it IS the engine's meta.
2. **The needs filter honours flex routes by construction** — it filters on `slotId ∪ alsoFits`, so a needed slot reachable by a cheap flex shows up exactly when the board's own chips say so. The Owner's "let the user try the weird flex" reads here as: the filter never hides a legal route to a needed slot.
3. **LEAD Δ marks** — the feed marks every drive that flipped or first broke the lead, computed from the running score (a pure, engine-true derivation). A fan re-reading a game gets the turning points without the box score.
4. **The provenance stamp on the feed** — "gameSeed N · week 1 · AWAY @ HOME · played by the pinned engine" printed under the FINAL: the witness-gate's spirit extended to a screen whose whole content is one engine run. The battery asserts the stamp's shape.
5. **The hydrated card names its own epistemology** — "trusted: ratings, roster, scores, plan · RE-DERIVED: table, seeding, bracket, champion" — so a user loading a save knows what the engine vouched for and what it recomputed. Corrupt-never-repair, made legible.
