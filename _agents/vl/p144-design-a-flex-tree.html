What is a flex?

A flex lets a player line up out of position — an edge rusher at DT, a safety at nickel, a tight end at receiver. Every flex has a cost in wins: the worse the fit, the bigger the penalty. Free flexes cost nothing; paid flexes show the wins you give up.

Who decides? You do.

The board never forces a flex. You see the penalty on every chip, and you choose whether to take it or keep looking. The engine never re-lineups your team on its own — advice only.

Where the wins come from

Wins come from picking the best available player at every slot, not from chasing raw OVR. A QB flexing to FS at -12 only makes sense when no better safety is on the board. The rankings you see are measured impact, not a salary table.
