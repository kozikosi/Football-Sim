METHOD — labelled (this is the §1.1 receipt fix; previously unlabelled, which invited a false defect):
  per-file figures = gzip -9 of each dist asset compressed SEPARATELY. The gzip stream of a named
                     file carries the asset's filename in its own header: +18 B for a .js name
                     (17 chars + flag), +19 B for the .css name.
  totals          = gzip -9 of the CONCATENATED js+css, piped via stdin (no filename header, one
                     stream, one dictionary). Per-file gzips therefore DO NOT SUM to the total and
                     must not be added: adding the columns gives 105,846 (vue) / 94,277 (svelte),
                     90 B / 80 B above the stated totals — 37 B of that is the two per-file name
                     headers, the rest is real compression gain from concatenating.
  Recompute either number byte-exactly:
    per-file:  gzip -9 -c <asset> | wc -c
    total:     cat <dist>/assets/*.js <dist>/assets/*.css | gzip -9 -c | wc -c
