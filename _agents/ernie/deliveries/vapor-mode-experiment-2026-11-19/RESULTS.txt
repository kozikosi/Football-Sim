--- the registered falsifier (filed 2026-11-15, inbox/20261115-1035-ernie-REVIEW-zai-fourslice.md) ---
THRESHOLD: "a Vue vapor build within 5 KB gz of Svelte on this surface"
MEASURED:  vapor is 17 KB gz OVER Svelte (31,498 - 13,154) — 3.4x the allowed gap
VERDICT:   FALSIFIED, decisively. Vapor does not close the gap; it widens it.

--- why (VERIFIED, read out of the emitted bundle) ---
1. Vapor IS genuinely engaged. The compiled component carries `__vapor:!0`, clones from a
   `gc()`-built template, and drives the DOM through `ss()` (renderEffect). There are ZERO
   `createVNode` / `createBlock` / `openBlock` calls in the bundle. This is real vapor codegen.
2. The component begins at byte 82,514 of an 83,040-byte bundle: 99.3% of the emitted size is
   the VAPOR RUNTIME, not the app. The runtime is ~66 KB raw and does not shrink with
   `optionsAPI:false` (measured byte-identical 83,040 B both ways).
3. runtime-vapor is a SECOND renderer alongside runtime-dom. `createApp`/`hydrate` are both
   present, so a mixed app pays for both.
4. Vapor's intended win is runtime DOM elimination, not bundle size. On this surface it costs
   +19.8% gz over vdom with no runtime benefit demonstrated (I did not measure runtime).

--- caveats, stated plainly ---
- Vue 3.6 is PRE-RELEASE (rc.15). Vapor is not officially complete. Numbers may move at 3.6.0
  final; the direction (bigger, not smaller) is unlikely to reverse but the magnitude may.
- Single-component micro-surface, not a 4-slice app. A real app amortizes the runtime over more
  components, which narrows the RATIO but cannot make vapor smaller than vdom on the same pair.
- No runtime perf measured. The case for vapor was always bundle size; that case is now closed.
- Svelte's 13,154 B gz is for the same surface and is the floor the falsifier referenced.

--- the one thing worth remembering ---
The plugin-level `vapor: true` silently did nothing (byte-identical output). Only the per-file
`<script setup vapor>` marker engaged. If anyone re-runs this, that is the trap: check for
`__vapor:!0` in the emitted chunk before trusting a number.
