Z-POST-BLUE — "20-0" rose-gold football sim
============================================

This folder is ready to go live in under a minute:

NETLIFY (easiest)
  1. Go to https://app.netlify.com/drop
  2. Drag THIS ENTIRE FOLDER onto the page
  3. Done — index.html is the whole app. No build step, no dependencies.

Any other static host works the same way (GitHub Pages, Cloudflare Pages,
Vercel static): serve this folder as-is; index.html is self-contained.

The standalone copy of the same app is named Football-Sim-Z-post-blue.html
(identical bytes to index.html) — you can also just double-click that file
to play locally in any modern browser.

What you will see
-----------------
- Product identity: "20-0" (title, masthead, footer, copy) with moving
  20-0s drifting across the header of the home screen
- A stripped, panel-free home stage: pick your team and draft slot, then
  press the shimmering DRAFT button
- The guide is hidden until you scroll down toward the footer, where it
  fades in from the ether
- Pick a team and start the draft: the whole app re-tints to your team's
  three brand colors (with tones adjusted for readability)
- During the draft, every player on the board is framed by his real NFL
  team's three colors; after the draft your roster wears your colors and
  other franchises' players wear theirs
- The draft board says "Flex WR (-4)" — no "Fits as" anywhere
- Light mode: pale rose-gold near-white with shimmering gold and white
- Dark mode: espresso palette; text is always champagne/cream (never black)
- The moon/sun button in the bottom pill switches light/dark and remembers
  your choice
- Player browser shows 15 players per page; click any column header to sort
- Click the "20-0" wordmark (top-left) to return to the home screen

Enjoy the chase for 20-0.
