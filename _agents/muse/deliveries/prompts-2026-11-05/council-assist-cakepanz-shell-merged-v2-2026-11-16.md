This prompt asks you to **assist that process in any way you can**: review the shell, define the seam, propose what is missing, verify the neutrality, and help with the blocked pieces. Your job in this round is to help the shell get built neutral, complete, and ready for the engine — not to re-litigate the framework pick.

**The shell, in one paragraph.** 67 Cakepanz is Z.ai's parallel UI track: the screens and flows of the product (Team Select, the draft board, the week stepper, the season book, the player cards, the live feed, the stats table, the settings form, the "my needs" row, the gone-odds tooltip, hydration and save), beautiful in both light and dark modes like w89, with the team driving the palette in both modes, offline-first, booting from vanilla file://. The engine stays the Architect's, single-sourced, consumed by the shell as a frozen snapshot at the current pin (`d3bd81d2`) through a single documented seam (the consumption layer). The shell must NOT contain engine logic or duplicate engine math. The user sees only three numbers: Wins, Priority, and OVR. The latest Cakepanz state is the `67-cakepanz-s5` delivery on council/inkling and the `67-cakepanz-s3-s4-rev2` delivery on council/ernie, plus the gh-pages deployment.
