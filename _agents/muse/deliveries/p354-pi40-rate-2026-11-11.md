(`~/workspace/nflverse-phase1/raw/play_by_play_*.parquet`, 462,773 REG plays).

`~/workspace/nflverse-phase1/out/dpi40_2016-2025.csv`.

## Headline (the number to use if the engine wants a real rate)

- **128** defensive pass interferences of 40+ yards in 10 REG seasons.
- **0.0243 per team-game** — one every **41 team-games** (≈ once every 2.4 seasons for a given team).
- **0.0485 per game** — one every ~21 games; **12.8 per season** (≈ 3 every 4 weeks league-wide).
- **0.00066 per pass attempt** — one every **1,509 pass attempts** (0.66 per 1,000).
- **4.7% of all DPI** are 40+ yards. 0.39% of all enforced penalties.

## Method

`penalty_type == 'Defensive Pass Interference'` and `penalty_yards >= 40`, REG only,
enforced only (`penalty_yards > 0`). Team-games = distinct REG game_ids × 2 (2,639
games, 5,278 team-games — matches the P-344 REG totals). A 40+ yard PI is a spot foul,
so `penalty_yards` is the foul yardage; no air-yards join was needed.
