With a 1,953-player pool, a 14–24 OVR floor below best-available never runs out of acceptable players — the floor does not bind, so CPU pick quality (and the user's edge) barely moves, and league parity never leaves ~2.17 win-SD (the 3.0–3.5 band is untouched too). The gap to 80/25/10 is a draft-quality gap: the naive drafter proves the engine can express 89.5% playoffs.
