6. **P16** — the lineup slot-picker chip still reads `−10 FLEX` (it contrasts NATURAL vs flex there); only the fills category was named. Say the word and the picker follows.
7. **P10** — the round cards cover the rounds played from the point SIM PLAYOFFS is pressed; rounds already watched round-by-round do not replay.
